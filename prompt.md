# Prompt: the meals folder — nutritious food, fast, anywhere

> Voice-memo dictation, captured 2026-06-27, lightly tidied and kept raw on
> purpose. The seed for this folder. Created alongside an `art/` folder, and
> promoted to its own root folder on 2026-06-27.

I want to figure out meals that are nutritious, easy — actually *quick* — to
make, and that rely on widely available ingredients.

"Easy" isn't the right word; *quick* is. And quick means a few things: the time
cooking, but also the time spent washing (some recipes just need a lot of
washing — anything with a blender), really the time that needs my *attention*.
Time to completion matters too: I can't imagine paying attention to something
twice in the span of four hours.

Widely available should also mean no specialised equipment — something I could do
at any Airbnb. And tasty, if we can get it — that's another criterion, ideal but
not required.

Subfolders: `ingredients`, `recipes`, maybe more.

For ingredients, I'd dispatch a workflow to produce a ranked table — rank by
nutritiousness, by how widely available, and so on. Some top contenders, each
with a short sentence on why it's where it is. I want to see the go-to spices,
the go-to oils — a bit like *Salt Fat Acid Heat*, what fills each category — plus
the vegetables, the fruits, and so on. I think I want just *one* oil (olive oil,
unless there's another widely-available healthy one to replace it with).

On availability: don't worry about price, worry about how widely available
something is. Anchor on the UK, but ideally weight for widely-available across the
world — the West and maybe Asia. Probably not travelling to Africa soon.

For recipes, they derive from the same principles: use the best ingredients by the
"widely available + nutritious + quick + tasty" criteria. Some I already make:

- **Spinach, tomato, and canned chickpeas** with olive oil and garlic. Dead
  simple — even with no spices it's tasty. Pretty nutritious; could be better.
- The same **with pasta** (the screw-shaped kind — fusilli — better than
  spaghetti, which doesn't fit the pot and makes a mess).
- **Kale and mushrooms** (iterating on this): blanch the kale, put the mushrooms
  in the same pot so they steam a little, then sauté the result with olive oil
  and garlic.

Notice these keep landing on a core set — olive oil, garlic. That's the point:
find the go-to staples and build on them.
