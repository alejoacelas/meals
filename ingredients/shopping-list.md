# Shopping list — a week for two

A week of food for two, built from the proven ingredients (`proven/`). Rough
quantities — tune to appetite. Grows as more categories get promoted.

## Produce
- Spinach — 2 large bags (or 1 fresh + 1 frozen)
- Kale — 2 bags
- Tomatoes — 6–8 fresh
- Mushrooms — 2 × 250 g
- Bell peppers — 4–5, mixed colours
- Broccoli — 2 heads
- Carrots — 1 kg bag
- Cabbage — 1 (keeps for weeks)
- Pak choi — 2–3
- Onions — 1 kg bag
- Garlic — 2 bulbs

## Fruit
- Bananas — 1 bunch
- Avocados — 3–4
- Apples — 6
- Oranges / clementines — 1 bag
- Strawberries — 1–2 punnets
- Grapes — 1 bag
- Frozen mixed berries — 1 large bag

## Protein
- Eggs — 18
- Greek yoghurt — 1 large tub (~1 kg)
- Firm tofu — 2 blocks
- Tinned sardines — 3 tins
- Tinned mackerel — 3 tins
- Chickpeas — 4–5 tins
- Red split lentils — 1 bag (500 g)

## Cupboard
- Pasta, fusilli/penne — 2 × 500 g
- Rolled oats — 1 kg bag
- Chopped tinned tomatoes — 2 tins
- Extra virgin olive oil — 1 bottle (lasts beyond a week)
- Rapeseed/canola oil — 1 bottle (lasts beyond a week)

## Bread & wraps
- Wholemeal / seeded sliced bread — 1 loaf
- Wholemeal wraps — 1 pack
- Wholemeal pita — 1 pack

## Spices — a starter rack (proven but not yet reviewed)
Buy the set once; it lasts ages. How to use them: `notes/using-spices.md`.
- Cumin, black pepper, chilli flakes, garlic granules, smoked paprika, turmeric,
  oregano, ground coriander, curry powder, cinnamon.

## Acids & seasoning
- Lemons — 3–4 (proven)
- Red wine vinegar — 1 bottle, lasts months (proven)
- Sea salt — restock if low (the one category still not promoted)

## A week of meals from this
- Spinach–tomato–chickpeas, on its own or over pasta
- Kale & mushroom sauté, with a fried egg or white beans
- Greek yoghurt + frozen berries + banana for breakfast
- Sardines or mackerel on toast, with sliced tomato
- Tofu or egg + veg stir-fry (pak choi, pepper, carrot), cumin + turmeric
- Red lentil dal with cumin, turmeric and garlic granules
