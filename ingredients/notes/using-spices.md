# Using spices

The whole blocker is "I don't know how they work." Here's the working knowledge —
two techniques, what each spice does, and a few no-measure combos. Don't memorise
it; cook from the combos at the bottom and the rest sticks.

## Two techniques cover almost everything
- **Bloom in oil (best).** Heat a little oil, add ground spices for ~30 seconds
  until they smell toasty, *then* add veg/beans/liquid. Heat wakes them up — the
  single biggest upgrade. Do this with cumin, turmeric, coriander, curry powder,
  paprika.
- **Sprinkle in.** Straight onto the food as it cooks or after. Fine for oregano,
  cinnamon, chilli flakes, black pepper, garlic granules.
- **Whole, slow.** Bay leaves go in at the start of a simmer and get fished out;
  worth it only in something you cook 20+ minutes.

Rule of thumb: **ground spices early (they need heat); black pepper and leafy dried
herbs late** (heat dulls them). Start with ~½ tsp per portion and adjust.

## What each one does
- **Cumin** — warm, earthy; the backbone of beans, chilli and curry. Your most-used jar.
- **Smoked paprika** — smoky depth; makes beans and eggs taste rich and cooked-down. (Sweet paprika = milder, no smoke.)
- **Turmeric** — earthy, mild, mostly colour; the yellow in curry. Bloom it, and pair with black pepper (which makes its curcumin far more absorbable).
- **Ground coriander** — warm, citrusy; cumin's partner. Use ~1:1 with cumin for an instant curry base.
- **Curry powder** — a ready blend (mostly turmeric + cumin + coriander). The lazy one-jar curry; bloom a heaped tsp in oil.
- **Chilli flakes** — heat by the pinch. Early for background warmth, at the end for a sharper hit.
- **Black pepper** — universal lift; grind on at the end.
- **Garlic granules** — garlic flavour, zero chopping; good in rubs or when you're out of fresh.
- **Oregano** — the Mediterranean note for anything with tomato; crumble it in.
- **Cinnamon** — sweet-warm; oats and fruit, but also a pinch in tomato/bean stews for depth.
- **Ground ginger** — warm bite for stir-fries and curries when there's no fresh root.
- **Bay leaf** — quiet background savouriness in long-simmered lentils and beans.

## No-measure combos to start with
- **Any pot of beans/chickpeas:** bloom cumin + smoked paprika + garlic granules in oil, add the beans and tomato, finish with chilli flakes.
- **10-minute dal:** bloom cumin + turmeric (+ a pinch of ginger), add red lentils and water, simmer; black pepper at the end.
- **Curry, fast:** bloom curry powder (or cumin + coriander + turmeric), add veg + tofu/chickpeas + a splash of water or yoghurt.
- **Greens (kale/spinach/pak choi):** garlic + chilli flakes in oil, then the greens; squeeze of lemon.
- **Eggs:** smoked paprika + black pepper; chilli flakes if you want heat.
- **Breakfast oats/yoghurt:** cinnamon, with berries and banana.

## Buying
A basic rack covers it: cumin, black pepper, chilli flakes, garlic granules,
smoked paprika, turmeric, oregano, ground coriander, curry powder, cinnamon. Cheap,
and they last months — buy small jars so they stay fresh. We'll prune the rack to
favourites once the recipes show which ones you actually reach for.
