# Legumes & Beans — candidates

Other legumes considered. The go-tos — canned chickpeas and red split lentils —
live in `../proven/legumes-and-beans.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Canned cannellini / white beans | Creamy enough to mash with a fork (no blender) for an instant side or dip. | 🟢 mash, stews, salads | 🟢 rinse, 0 cook | 🟢 | 🟢 protein, fibre, folate, potassium |
| 2 | Canned kidney beans | UK-ubiquitous and the backbone of a fast chili; loses points only on a narrower dish range. | 🟢 chili, salads, rice bowls | 🟢 rinse, 0 cook | 🟢 | 🟢 protein, fibre, iron, folate |
| 3 | Canned black beans | Brilliant for Tex-Mex and tops common beans for antioxidants, but not on every UK shelf. | 🟢 tacos, bowls, soups | 🟢 rinse, 0 cook | 🟡 big supermarkets | 🟢 protein, fibre, folate, antioxidants |
| 4 | Canned butter / lima beans | Big and buttery, great gently braised; a British favourite but less common canned elsewhere. | 🟢 stews, mash, braises | 🟢 rinse, 0 cook | 🟡 UK staple, patchier elsewhere | 🟢 fibre, potassium, protein |
| 5 | Frozen edamame | Rare complete-protein legume and quick to boil, but freezer-only and less universal. | 🟡 snack, stir-fry, bowls | 🟢 boil 4 min | 🟡 supermarket freezers/Asian | 🟢 complete protein, folate, vit K |
| 6 | Frozen peas | Always-on freezer staple that sneaks protein and colour into any dish in minutes. | 🟢 sides, rice, pasta, soups | 🟢 boil/steam 3 min | 🟢 | 🟢 fibre, vit C, vit K, protein |
| 7 | Canned green / brown lentils | Holds shape better than red lentils with zero cooking; just a touch less common tinned. | 🟢 salads, stews, ragu | 🟢 rinse, 0 cook | 🟡 most supermarkets | 🟢 protein, fibre, iron |
| 8 | Canned baked beans | Effortless and everywhere, but sugar and salt drag the nutrition down a peg. | 🟡 toast, fry-ups | 🟢 heat 3 min | 🟢 | 🟡 fibre, protein (added sugar/salt) |
| 9 | Dried puy / green lentils | No soak needed, but the longer simmer makes the can the smarter buy. | 🟢 salads, sides | 🟡 25–30 min, no soak | 🟡 | 🟢 protein, fibre, iron |
| 10 | Dried kidney / cannellini / chickpeas | Same beans, but the overnight soak is a second attention episode — buy the tin. | 🟢 same as canned | 🔴 overnight soak + 1 hr | 🟢 | 🟢 protein, fibre, iron |

**Notes:**
- Always rinse canned beans to cut sodium.
- Dried red kidney beans must be boiled hard ~10 min to destroy lectins — canned are pre-cooked and safe, another reason to favour cans.
- Availability — black beans and tinned green lentils are 🟡 (larger supermarkets); butter beans are a UK staple but patchier elsewhere; edamame is freezer-aisle or Asian-grocer only.
- Honourable mentions: firm tofu (now proven — see `../proven/other-proteins.md`), tinned lentil/bean soups for zero-effort days, mung beans (🟡 outside Asian shops).
