# Grains & Starches — candidates

Other grains and starches considered. The go-tos — pasta (fusilli/penne) and rolled
oats — live in `../proven/grains-and-starches.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Couscous | Fastest grain here — no active cooking, just steep and fluff; held back only on Asian availability. | 🟢 salads, sides, bowls | 🟢 pour boiling water, 5 min off heat | 🟡 staple in UK/West, specialty in Asia | 🟡 carbs, modest protein/fibre |
| 2 | White / basmati rice | Most universally available staple; loses points only on refined nutrition and timing. | 🟢 base for everything | 🟡 12–15 min, watch the timing | 🟢 universal, esp. Asia | 🟡 mostly carbs; minimal micronutrients |
| 3 | Potato | Genuinely nutritious and versatile; ranked here for knife work and a longer boil. | 🟢 boil, mash, pan-fry | 🟡 peel/chop + 15–20 min boil | 🟢 | 🟢 potassium, vit C, fibre (skin on) |
| 4 | Quinoa | Top nutrition, but pricier, less available, and a touch slower than the leaders. | 🟢 bowls, salads, sides | 🟡 15 min simmer, rinse first | 🟡 West yes, patchier in Asia | 🟢 near-complete protein, magnesium, iron |
| 5 | Bulgur wheat | Fast and wholegrain-nutritious; held back by narrower availability. | 🟡 pilafs, salads (tabbouleh) | 🟢 ~12 min or steep | 🟡 common UK/Med, less in Asia | 🟢 fibre, manganese, protein |
| 6 | Sweet potato | Excellent nutrition; ranked down as it's slower, knife-heavy and usually oven-roasted. | 🟡 mash, cubes, fry | 🟡 chop + 15–20 min boil or pan-fry | 🟢 | 🟢 vit A (beta-carotene), potassium, fibre |
| 7 | Rice noodles | Very fast and great for Asian dishes, but thin on nutrition. | 🟡 stir-fries, soups | 🟢 soak/boil 3–5 min | 🟡 strong in Asia, OK in UK | 🔴 mostly refined carbs |
| 8 | Egg noodles | Quick and handy, but nutritionally unremarkable. | 🟡 stir-fries, soups | 🟢 4–6 min boil | 🟢 | 🟡 carbs + a little protein |
| 9 | Polenta (cornmeal) | Quick-cook exists but the babysitting and constant stirring drop it down. | 🟡 soft or set-and-fry | 🔴 needs near-constant stirring | 🟡 common in West | 🟡 carbs, trace carotenoids |
| 10 | Brown rice | More nutritious than white rice, but the long cook fails the "fast" test. | 🟢 base for everything | 🔴 25–40 min simmer | 🟢 | 🟢 fibre, magnesium, manganese |

**Notes:**
- Wholewheat versions of pasta, couscous and bulgur upgrade nutrition (more fibre/protein) at no extra effort — buy them when available.
- Rinse quinoa to cut bitter saponins (pre-rinsed packs skip this).
- Skip anything needing overnight soaking (dried beans, steel-cut oats) — it breaks the one-session rule.
- Availability — couscous, bulgur and quinoa are Western staples but patchier across East/SE Asia; rice noodles, egg noodles and polenta are easiest at larger UK supermarkets.
- Honourable mentions: pre-cooked rice/grain pouches (2 min in the pan, pricier), corn tortillas/wraps as a no-cook starch, soba (buckwheat noodles, ~5 min, decent protein/magnesium).
