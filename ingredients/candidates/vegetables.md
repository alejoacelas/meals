# Vegetables — candidates

Vegetables considered but not in the everyday set. The proven vegetables live in
`../proven/vegetables.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Courgette | Mild, fast-frying filler; lower nutrient density than the proven greens, so it's optional. | 🟢 | 🟢 slice, 4–5 min | 🟢 | 🟡 vit C, potassium |

**Notes:**
- Onion, garlic and spring onion are vegetables too, but they belong with the aromatic base — see `../proven/aromatics.md` and `aromatics.md`.
- Frozen spinach/peas and tinned chopped tomatoes are honourable mentions, kept as pantry backups (see the proven vegetables).
