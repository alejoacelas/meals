# Acids — candidates

Other acids considered. The go-tos — fresh lemon and red wine vinegar — live in
`../proven/acids.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fresh lime | The lemon of the East/Latin kitchen; non-negotiable for Thai and taco-style food. | 🟢 SE-Asian, Mexican, curries | 🟢 halve + squeeze | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C (about half a lemon's), flavonoids |
| 2 | Rice vinegar | Mild and slightly sweet so it brightens without dominating; keeps indefinitely. | 🟢 stir-fry finish, quick pickles, dressings | 🟢 just pour | 🟡 most UK + Asian shops | 🔴 negligible (acetic acid) |
| 3 | Apple cider vinegar | Fruity-sharp workhorse with mild blood-sugar upside, though its assertive flavour limits delicate dishes. | 🟡 slaws, braises, dressings | 🟢 just pour | 🟢 | 🔴 negligible; trace potassium |
| 4 | White wine vinegar | Cleaner and more neutral than red — the safe choice when you don't want colour or sweetness. | 🟢 dressings, pan sauces, poaching | 🟢 just pour | 🟢 | 🔴 negligible |
| 5 | Balsamic vinegar | Sweet depth that reduces to a glaze in the pan, but the sugar narrows its savoury range. | 🟡 salads, tomatoes, quick glaze | 🟢 just pour | 🟢 | 🔴 negligible; sugars + polyphenols |
| 6 | Bottled lemon/lime juice | The no-knife, no-waste citrus standby; a real finishing acid when fresh fruit isn't around, though flavour and vit C are duller. | 🟡 cooking backup | 🟢 just pour | 🟢 | 🟡 some vit C, less than fresh |
| 7 | Distilled white vinegar | Cheapest and sharpest acid going, but too harsh and one-note for most finishing. | 🔴 pickling, cleaning | 🟢 just pour | 🟢 | 🔴 none |
| 8 | Malt vinegar | A UK staple with real character but a narrow remit, and scarce outside Britain. | 🟡 chips, pickles, slaws | 🟢 just pour | 🟢 UK, 🟡 elsewhere | 🔴 negligible |
| 9 | Tamarind concentrate | Unique sweet-sour fruit acid that earns its place in specific dishes; buy paste/concentrate, not the soak-it block. | 🟡 pad thai, curries, chutney | 🟢 spoon + dissolve | 🟡 Asian aisle / shops | 🟡 potassium, magnesium, B vits |
| 10 | Sumac | Lemony acid you can sprinkle dry over salads, eggs and meats — handy but only ever a garnish. | 🟡 finishing dust, dressings | 🟢 sprinkle | 🟡 spice aisle / Middle-Eastern | 🟡 antioxidant-rich but only a sprinkle's worth |

**Notes:**
- Acids are flavour tools, not nutrition — only fresh citrus contributes much (lime ~half a lemon's vit C); don't rank vinegars on nutrition.
- Fresh citrus is the one perishable group; every vinegar keeps essentially forever — the most Airbnb-friendly pantry buy.
- Availability — rice vinegar, tamarind and sumac are everyday in East/SE-Asian and Middle-Eastern shops but 🟡 in a default UK supermarket; malt vinegar is the reverse (🟢 in Britain, scarce elsewhere).
- Honourable mentions: sherry vinegar (superb but specialty), pomegranate molasses (sweet-tart drizzle), amchur / dried mango powder (dry souring dust), caper/pickle brine (free acid from the jar).
