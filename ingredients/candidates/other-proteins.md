# Other Proteins — candidates

Other proteins considered. The go-tos — eggs, Greek yoghurt, tinned sardines &
mackerel, and firm tofu — live in `../proven/other-proteins.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tinned tuna | The most available and crowd-pleasing tin; leaner and lower-omega-3 than oily fish, watch mercury. | 🟢 salad, pasta, sandwich, melt | 🟢 open tin, no cook | 🟢 | 🟢 lean protein, selenium, niacin, B12 |
| 2 | Cottage cheese | High protein for the effort and calories; texture limits it to fewer dishes. | 🟡 on toast, with fruit, stirred into pasta | 🟢 spoon from tub, no cook | 🟢 | 🟢 casein protein (~11g/100g), calcium, B12 |
| 3 | Tinned salmon | Oily-fish nutrition with milder flavour than sardines; a touch pricier per tin. | 🟡 salad, fishcakes, on toast | 🟢 open tin, no cook | 🟢 | 🟢 omega-3, vit D, calcium (bones), B12 |
| 4 | Frozen cooked prawns | Lean high-protein seafood that's broadly stocked; a short thaw or quick-cook keeps it mid-table. | 🟢 stir-fry, pasta, curry, salad | 🟡 thaw 5 min or pan-fry from frozen 3–4 min | 🟢 | 🟢 lean protein (~24g/100g), B12, selenium, iodine, zinc |
| 5 | Halloumi | Fast pan-fry with great taste, but high salt and saturated fat cap the nutrition. | 🟡 fried slices, salad, wraps | 🟢 slice, pan-fry 4 min | 🟢 | 🟡 protein, calcium |
| 6 | Feta | No-cook flavour bomb that lifts many dishes; salty and only moderate protein. | 🟡 salad, eggs, pasta, wraps | 🟢 crumble, no cook | 🟢 | 🟡 protein, calcium, phosphorus |
| 7 | Edamame (frozen) | Genuinely nutritious plant protein, but needs the freezer and a quick boil. | 🟡 snack, salad, stir-in | 🟡 boil/steam 5 min | 🟡 frozen aisle, common in West/Asia | 🟢 plant protein, folate, fibre, vit K |
| 8 | Tempeh | Excellent fermented protein, now stocked across major UK chains; still needs cooking and isn't in every small shop. | 🟡 fried, stir-fry, sandwich | 🟡 slice, pan-fry 8 min | 🟡 major UK chains, larger branches | 🟢 fermented soy protein, fibre, probiotics, manganese |

**Notes:**
- Mind mercury with frequent tuna; favour the proven sardines/mackerel for omega-3 and bone-in calcium.
- Honourable mentions: cheddar and hard cheeses (no-cook protein + calcium, calorie-dense), paneer (🟡, quick pan-fry), skyr (like Greek yoghurt, more protein).
- Edamame also appears under legumes — same bean, freezer aisle.
