# Aromatics — candidates

Other aromatics considered. The base — garlic + onion — lives in
`../proven/aromatics.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spring onion | Near-zero prep and works raw as a garnish or cooked as a base, across Western and Asian plates alike. | 🟢 raw or cooked | 🟢 just slice, no peel | 🟢 | 🟢 vit K, vit C, folate |
| 2 | Ginger | Garlic's partner in every stir-fry and curry base; peeling and grating cost it the very top spots. | 🟢 stir-fries, curries, soups | 🟡 peel + grate | 🟢 | 🟢 gingerol, manganese |
| 3 | Coriander (cilantro) | A zero-cook finisher with huge reach, held back only by the soap-gene divide. | 🟢 cross-cuisine finisher | 🟢 just chop, raw | 🟢 | 🟢 vit K, vit A, vit C |
| 4 | Parsley (flat-leaf) | The most nutrient-dense everyday herb, scattered raw over almost anything green or beany. | 🟢 over greens, beans, eggs | 🟢 just chop, raw | 🟢 | 🟢 vit K, vit C, folate, iron |
| 5 | Fresh chilli | Easy slice of fresh heat, but dried chilli flakes do most of the same job from the cupboard. | 🟡 heat is optional | 🟢 slice, deseed optional | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C, capsaicin |
| 6 | Celery | The third leg of the Western mirepoix: near-zero prep and 🟢 everywhere, held back only by being a background base. | 🟡 soups, braises, stir-fry base | 🟢 just slice, no peel | 🟢 | 🟢 vit K, folate, potassium |
| 7 | Shallot | A milder, sweeter onion that shines raw in dressings; fiddly peels and patchier shelves drop it here. | 🟡 best raw in dressings | 🟡 fiddly small peels | 🟡 not every basic shop | 🟢 quercetin, antioxidants |
| 8 | Leek | A gentle onion base, but grit between layers means real washing and it needs longer to soften. | 🟡 soups, braises | 🟡 wash grit + slice, longer cook | 🟡 West-leaning | 🟢 vit K, folate, prebiotic fibre |
| 9 | Basil | Lovely but narrow and quick to wilt, so it earns fewer dishes. | 🟡 mostly Italian/Thai | 🟢 tear in at the end | 🟡 common in West | 🟢 vit K, antioxidants |
| 10 | Mint | Bright and healthy but limited mainly to yoghurt, salads and a few Asian plates. | 🟡 yoghurt, salads, some Asian | 🟢 chop or tear | 🟢 | 🟢 vit A, iron |
| 11 | Lemongrass | Stocked fresh by big UK chains, but woody — needs bruising and a long simmer, a poor fit for quick pot/pan cooking. | 🔴 a few SE-Asian dishes | 🔴 bruise, trim, long simmer | 🟡 big chains + Asian shops | 🔴 low density, citral oils |

**Notes:**
- Ginger and garlic sell as jarred paste that skips all prep, though fresh tastes noticeably better.
- Coriander splits people on a genetic soap taste — parsley is the safe swap, though parsley itself leans West.
- Availability — shallot, leek and basil are reliable across the West but patchier in East/SE-Asian supermarkets; lemongrass is the reverse.
- Red onion folds into onion (sweeter, better raw in salads).
- Dried herbs and spices (cumin, chilli flakes, black pepper) live in their own category.
