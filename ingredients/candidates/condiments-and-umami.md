# Condiments & Umami — candidates

Other condiments considered. The go-tos — soy sauce, tomato purée, stock/bouillon
paste, miso and fish sauce — live in `../proven/condiments-and-umami.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oyster sauce | Glossy, MSG-rich umami bomb that deepens stir-fries, glazes, noodles and even Western gravies. | 🟢 stir-fries, marinades, glazes, greens, gravies | 🟢 just spoon | 🟡 UK world-food aisle, 🟢 East/SE Asia | 🔴 small amounts; sugar + sodium; not veggie |
| 2 | Gochujang | Sweet-spicy-savoury fermented chilli paste, real fermented umami, at home in stews, glazes and noodles. | 🟡 stews, marinades, noodles, eggs, dressings | 🟢 spoon + stir | 🟡 UK chains, 🟢 Korea | 🔴 small amounts; sugar + sodium |
| 3 | Worcestershire sauce | A few drops of anchovy-tamarind-molasses umami deepen stews, mince and cheese on toast. | 🟡 stews, mince, marinades, cheese on toast | 🟢 just splash | 🟢 West, 🟡 Asia | 🔴 negligible; tiny amounts |
| 4 | Mustard (Dijon) | Pungent, tangy emulsifier — light on umami but a tiny spoon sharpens and binds any dressing or sauce. | 🟢 dressings, pan sauces, marinades, sandwiches | 🟢 just spoon | 🟢 West, 🟡 Asia | 🔴 small amounts; trace selenium |
| 5 | Harissa | Smoky North-African chilli paste that doubles as heat and depth across eggs, stews and dressings. | 🟡 stews, eggs, roasting, dressings | 🟢 just spoon | 🟡 common in West, patchier in Asia | 🟡 chilli antioxidants; small amounts |
| 6 | Hot sauce (Sriracha / Tabasco) | The near-universal finisher — heat and tang on almost any plate, though a topper more than a cooking base. | 🟢 finisher on almost anything | 🟢 just shake | 🟢 | 🔴 negligible; capsaicin, trace vit C |
| 7 | Ketchup | Sweet-tangy-umami that everyone has, but the sugar load and narrow cooking role keep it low. | 🟡 dipping, quick sauces, marinades | 🟢 just squeeze | 🟢 | 🔴 sugar-heavy; some lycopene |
| 8 | Yeast extract (Marmite) | Near-pure glutamate punch, but narrow, polarising and very-British in use and reach. | 🔴 toast, stock/gravy booster, stew depth | 🟢 tiny scrape / dissolve | 🟢 UK/ANZ, 🔴 elsewhere | 🟢 B-vitamins, added B12 + folate |

**Notes:**
- Availability — oyster sauce and gochujang are everyday in their home regions but only 🟡 in a default UK shop (world-food aisle); harissa is the reverse (common in the West, patchier in Asia); Marmite is a UK/ANZ thing, scarce elsewhere.
- Oyster sauce isn't vegetarian; mushroom ("vegetarian oyster") versions give most of the same glossy umami.
- Marmite is a near-pure umami/B-vitamin hit (fortified with B12) but polarising — a background booster, not a base.
- Honourable mentions: hoisin, nutritional yeast (vegan cheesy umami; see nutrition powerhouses), MSG (purest umami enhancer), anchovy paste, doubanjiang/chilli-bean paste, dashi/kombu.
