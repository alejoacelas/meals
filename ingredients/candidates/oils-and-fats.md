# Oils & Fats — candidates

Other oils and fats considered. The two go-tos — extra virgin olive oil and
rapeseed/canola — live in `../proven/oils-and-fats.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Butter | Great flavour and fat-soluble vitamins; capped only because saturated fat limits daily use. | 🟢 eggs, veg, sauces, toast | 🟢 cut a knob | 🟢 | 🟡 vit A, K2, butyrate; high saturated |
| 2 | Toasted sesame oil | A few drops transform Asian dishes; a finishing oil, not a cooking one. | 🟡 finish stir-fries, noodles, dressings | 🟢 drizzle | 🟡 West/Asian shops | 🟢 vit E, sesamin antioxidants |
| 3 | Ghee | Butter flavour with a high smoke point and long shelf life; same saturated-fat cap, slightly less ubiquitous. | 🟢 high-heat frying, dal, eggs | 🟢 spoon | 🟡 common via S. Asian stores | 🟡 vit A, E; high saturated, lactose-free |
| 4 | Groundnut/peanut oil | Clean flavour and a very high smoke point make it the classic wok oil. | 🟡 stir-fry, deep-fry | 🟢 pour | 🟡 supermarkets/Asian shops | 🟢 MUFA, vit E |
| 5 | Sunflower oil | Cheap and everywhere, but the omega-6 skew keeps it below rapeseed. | 🟢 neutral frying, baking | 🟢 pour | 🟢 | 🟡 vit E; omega-6 heavy |
| 6 | Coconut oil | Heat-stable with a distinctive flavour, but very high in saturated fat — occasional use. | 🟡 curries, frying, sweet dishes | 🟢 scoop | 🟢 | 🟡 mostly lauric acid; very high saturated |
| 7 | Avocado oil | Nutritionally olive-like and great for high heat, but cost and patchier stock rank it down. | 🟢 sear, dress, finish | 🟢 pour | 🟡 pricier shelves | 🟢 MUFA, vit E; high smoke point |
| 8 | Lard / beef dripping | Superb frying flavour and stability, but saturated fat and uneven stock cap it. | 🟡 roasting, frying, refried beans | 🟢 spoon | 🟡 butchers/supermarkets | 🟡 some vit D; high saturated |
| 9 | Flaxseed/linseed oil | Strong omega-3 case, but it can't be cooked and must be refrigerated — niche only. | 🔴 raw drizzle on cold food | 🔴 fridge-only, never heat | 🟡 health aisles/chilled | 🟢 highest plant omega-3 ALA |

**Notes:**
- Availability — toasted sesame oil, ghee and groundnut oil are reliable across the UK and East/SE Asia but lean on larger or Asian/South-Asian grocers; avocado oil is mainstream but pricey.
- Butter, ghee, coconut oil and lard are all high in saturated fat — occasional-use, not everyday.
- Coconut oil is ~half lauric acid, metabolised largely like a long-chain saturated fat, so the "MCT" health halo is overstated.
- Avocado oil's high smoke point is the refined product (virgin is ~190–205°C).
- Honourable mention: tahini (sesame paste) — similar fats plus calcium and protein, but a condiment, not a cooking fat.
