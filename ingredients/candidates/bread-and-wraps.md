# Bread & Wraps — candidates

Other bread and wrap options considered. The go-tos — wholemeal/seeded sliced
bread, wholemeal wraps, and wholemeal pita — live in
`../proven/bread-and-wraps.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sourdough | A superb sturdy base for tartines and the best-tasting toast here, but still white-flour nutrition and more artisan to find. | 🟢 toast, tartines, dippers | 🟢 no cook; toast optional | 🟡 bakery aisle, patchier | 🟡 refined white flour; only a genuine long ferment modestly lowers GI |
| 2 | Corn tortillas | Naturally wholegrain and the heart of tacos/tostadas, but they crack unless warmed and aren't on every shelf. | 🟡 tacos, tostadas | 🟡 warm 20s to soften | 🟡 big supermarkets/Latin | 🟡 wholegrain corn, fibre; nixtamal adds calcium/niacin |
| 3 | Wholemeal English muffins | Split-and-pile vehicle made for a stacked egg-and-bean breakfast; wholegrain option exists but availability is West-centric. | 🟡 split sandwiches, eggs | 🟢 split + toast | 🟡 UK/US staple, less in Asia | 🟡 fibre, some calcium, protein |
| 4 | Oatcakes | Zero-cook wholegrain cracker that's a natural raft for cheese, fish or smashed avocado — crumbly, fairly fatty/salty, and quite UK-centric. | 🟡 toppers, fish/avo rafts | 🟢 no cook; just top | 🟡 UK/Scottish staple | 🟢 wholegrain oats: beta-glucan fibre + protein (but ~18–20% fat and salted) |
| 5 | Rye / wholegrain crispbread (Ryvita) | Long-life, no-cook, very high-fibre cracker for quick open toppers — but snack-scale and dry rather than a real meal base. | 🟡 fish/avocado toppers | 🟢 no cook; just top | 🟡 UK/Europe, patchier in Asia | 🟢 very high rye fibre, low fat |
| 6 | Rye bread | Dense and properly high-fibre, brilliant for fish/avocado open sandwiches, but the flavour is divisive and it's a Northern-Europe item. | 🟡 open sandwiches | 🟢 no cook; slice and top | 🟡 N. Europe staple, larger UK stores | 🟢 high fibre, manganese, low GI |
| 7 | Flatbread / naan | A handy scoop-and-wrap for beans and eggs, but usually refined, best warmed, and fresh stock is patchy. | 🟡 scoops, wraps | 🟡 warm to soften | 🟡 UK/South Asia, patchier elsewhere | 🟡 mostly refined carbs (wholemeal better) |

**Notes:**
- Everything here is genuinely no-cook; "warm 20s" for corn tortilla/naan is optional softening, not real cooking.
- Sourdough's GI edge is overstated: the fermentation effect matters mainly for *wholegrain* loaves, and supermarket "sourdough" is often *sourfaux* (flavoured, not truly fermented) — it earns its rank on versatility, not nutrition.
- Oatcakes are wholegrain and high-fibre but ~18–20% fat plus added salt — a solid topper, not a health hero.
- Corn tortillas are the gluten-free pick; oatcakes and rye crispbread keep for weeks — the good store-cupboard backup when fresh bread runs out.
- Honourable mentions: chapati/roti (wholewheat atta, the most Asia-available flatbread, but needs a quick pan-warm), bagels (dense, versatile, but refined and heavy).
