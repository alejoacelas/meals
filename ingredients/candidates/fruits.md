# Fruits — candidates

Fruit considered but not in the everyday set. The proven fruit lives in
`../proven/fruits.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kiwi | More vit C than orange and eaten with a spoon — a great fruit, just not a staple I reach for. | 🟡 snack, fruit bowl | 🟢 halve and spoon | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C (very high), vit K, fibre |
| 2 | Blueberries (fresh) | Among the top antioxidant fruits, but frozen mixed berries already deliver this year-round and cheaper. | 🟡 oats, yogurt, snack | 🟢 rinse | 🟡 seasonal/pricey | 🟢 antioxidants, vit K, vit C |
| 3 | Mango | Excellent nutrition and taste, but the fiddly stone and hit-or-miss ripeness are too much prep. | 🟡 snack, salsa, yogurt | 🟡 knife, awkward stone | 🟡 ripeness varies | 🟢 vit C, vit A, folate |
| 4 | Pineapple (fresh) | Great flavour but the heaviest prep here — tinned in juice is the fast shortcut. | 🟡 snack, salsa, stir-fry | 🔴 heavy peel/core | 🟡 | 🟡 manganese (high), vit C, bromelain |

**Notes:**
- Lemon and lime are fruits too, but they live with the acids — see `acids.md`.
- For blueberries' antioxidants without the price/season, buy the proven frozen mixed berries.
- Honourable mentions: pomegranate (great nutrition, too fiddly to seed) and raspberries (lovely but delicate and pricey).
