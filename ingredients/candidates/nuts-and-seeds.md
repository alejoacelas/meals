# Nuts & Seeds — candidates

Other nuts and seeds considered. The go-tos — peanut butter, almonds and pumpkin
seeds — live in `../proven/nuts-and-seeds.md`.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tahini | A mineral-dense no-cook sauce base; mainly the backbone of hummus, but also dressings and drizzles. | 🟢 hummus, dressings, drizzle on veg/grains | 🟢 stir, loosen with water + lemon | 🟢 | 🟢 iron (~9mg/100g), copper, healthy fats, protein |
| 2 | Walnuts | The top nut for plant omega-3 and antioxidants; a little goes a long way. | 🟡 salads, oats, snack, chopped topping | 🟢 crush by hand, no cook | 🟢 | 🟢 omega-3 ALA, copper, manganese, polyphenols |
| 3 | Mixed nuts | Broad mineral and healthy-fat coverage in one convenient bag. | 🟡 snack, chopped topping | 🟢 pour, no cook | 🟢 | 🟢 mixed MUFA, magnesium, vit E; selenium only if it contains Brazil nuts |
| 4 | Hemp seeds (hearts) | Complete plant protein with good omega ratio; only the health-aisle stock holds it back. | 🟢 sprinkle on anything savoury or sweet | 🟢 sprinkle, no cook | 🟡 health aisle / larger shops | 🟢 complete protein (~10g/30g), omega-3, magnesium |
| 5 | Chia seeds | Omega-3, fibre and calcium; best stirred into yoghurt or oats, or soaked into pudding. | 🟡 puddings, stir into yoghurt/oats, thicken | 🟢 stir in, or soak 10 min | 🟢 | 🟢 omega-3 ALA, fibre, calcium |
| 6 | Sunflower seeds | Cheap, everywhere and vitamin-E rich — a workhorse sprinkle just below pumpkin. | 🟢 sprinkle, snack, salads | 🟢 sprinkle, no cook | 🟢 | 🟢 vit E, selenium, magnesium; omega-6 heavy |
| 7 | Cashews | Creamy and tasty, but lower fibre and more starch than the other nuts. | 🟡 snack, stir-fry topping, butter | 🟢 handful, no cook | 🟢 | 🟡 magnesium, copper, iron; less fibre, more carb |
| 8 | Peanuts (roasted) | Cheap protein and niacin; technically a legume but used like a nut. | 🟡 snack, stir-fry/noodle topping, satay | 🟢 handful, no cook | 🟢 | 🟢 protein, niacin, folate, resveratrol |
| 9 | Ground flaxseed | Strong omega-3 and lignan case, but only counts if ground — whole seeds pass through. | 🟡 stir into oats/yoghurt, bind | 🟡 buy ground, or grind if whole | 🟡 health aisle / chilled | 🟢 omega-3 ALA, lignans, fibre |
| 10 | Sesame seeds | Nutritious in theory but eaten by the pinch — use tahini for a real dose. | 🟡 garnish, toast for nuttiness | 🟢 sprinkle, no cook | 🟢 | 🟡 calcium/iron but small amounts |

**Notes:**
- Availability — tahini is mainstream across UK/Western chains; in East/SE Asia the equivalent is Chinese/Japanese sesame paste. Hemp hearts and ground flax lean on the health aisle or larger branches. Everything else is true 🟢.
- Tahini's main use is hummus (proven — see `../proven/other-proteins.md`); on its own, loosen it with warm water, lemon and salt for a no-cook dressing. Most supermarket tahini is hulled (less calcium, ~60–130mg/100g); buy unhulled for a real calcium dose.
- Chia, flax and hemp also belong with nutrition powerhouses — keep them wherever you actually reach for them.
- Storage — nut butters, tahini and oily seeds (flax, hemp, walnuts) go rancid; buy small, keep cool, refrigerate flax and tahini once open.
- Honourable mentions — almond/cashew butter (the peanut-butter role, pricier), pistachios (protein, fibre, B6, lutein), Brazil nuts (one a day covers selenium), pine nuts (great but expensive).
