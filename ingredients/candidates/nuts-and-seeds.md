# Nuts & Seeds — candidates

Ranked options for a new category, none promoted yet — review and move the keepers into `../proven/`. Dominant criterion: **nutrition (healthy fats, protein, minerals) + no-cook convenience**; taste is high across the board, so it barely separates these. Read with the weighting rule in `../../CLAUDE.md` in mind.

**Notes:**
- Availability — tahini is mainstream across UK/Western chains; in East/SE Asia the equivalent is Chinese/Japanese sesame paste. Hemp hearts and ground flax lean on the health aisle or larger branches. Everything else is true 🟢.
- Tahini calcium — most supermarket tahini is hulled, with far less calcium (~60–130mg/100g) than the headline figures suggest; only unhulled/whole-seed tahini is genuinely calcium-dense (~900mg+/100g, though much of it as less-absorbable oxalate). Its reliably high minerals are iron and copper. Buy unhulled if you want it as a calcium source.
- Protein reality check — peanut butter is a great spoonable protein, but per 100g it's matched or beaten by hemp seeds (~32g), peanuts (~26g) and pumpkin seeds; it leads on convenience and ubiquity, not raw protein density.
- Cross-references — chia, flax and hemp also belong with nutrition powerhouses; keep them wherever you actually reach for them, listed once if you split that category out.
- Storage — nut butters, tahini and any ground/oily seeds (flax, hemp, walnuts) go rancid; buy small, keep cool, and refrigerate flax and tahini once open.
- Prep tip — loosen tahini or peanut butter with warm water, lemon and a pinch of salt for an instant no-cook dressing; toast seeds dry in the one pan for 2 min to wake up flavour.
- Honourable mentions — almond butter and cashew butter (same role as peanut butter, pricier, patchier stock); pistachios (nutritionally a notch above cashews — protein, fibre, B6, lutein — and arguably a ranked spot) and Brazil nuts (one a day covers selenium, but easy to overdo); pine nuts (great taste, expensive, niche).
- Peanut allergy caveat — peanut butter and peanuts are off the table in shared/allergic settings; almonds or pumpkin seeds cover the same role.
