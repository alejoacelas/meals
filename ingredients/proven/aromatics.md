# Proven — Aromatics

The aromatic base. Other options considered are in `../candidates/aromatics.md`.

**Dominant criterion: versatility + flavour-per-prep.** Judged on how many dishes
they anchor and the flavour they return for the prep — not nutrition (you don't
eat aromatics for the vitamins).

## Garlic + onion — the flavour foundation
- **Why proven:** Together they're the base of nearly every savoury pan dish here.
  Onion softened first builds the body; garlic stirred in for the last 30 seconds
  adds depth without burning. Both keep for weeks unrefrigerated, cost almost
  nothing, and are 🟢 available everywhere.
- **Use for:** the start of essentially every recipe — soften onion in oil, add
  garlic late.
- **Shortcut:** jarred garlic paste skips the peel-and-mince when you're in a
  hurry, though fresh tastes better.
