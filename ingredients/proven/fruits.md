# Proven — Fruits

The everyday fruit, for raw eating or near-zero prep. Other options considered are
in `../candidates/fruits.md`.

**Dominant criterion: nutrition + near-zero prep.** Fruit here is mostly a raw
snack or breakfast add-in, so it's picked on nutrient density and grab-and-go ease;
anything that needs real knife work drops out.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Banana | Cheapest, most universal, zero-prep energy that pairs with everything. | 🟢 snack, oats, pancake | 🟢 zero, just peel | 🟢 | 🟢 potassium, vit B6, quick carbs |
| 2 | Avocado | Best nutrition here and no blender needed for guac; only minor knife work. | 🟢 toast, salad, eggs | 🟢 knife, mash with fork | 🟢 | 🟢 monounsat fat, fibre, potassium, folate |
| 3 | Frozen mixed berries | Year-round, cheaper than fresh, and a freezer staple in any flat. | 🟢 oats, yogurt, defrost-compote | 🟢 spoon from bag | 🟢 freezer aisle | 🟢 anthocyanins, vit C, fibre |
| 4 | Orange / clementine | Portable vit C bomb; clementines need not even a knife. | 🟡 snack, salad, juice | 🟢 peel by hand | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C, folate, fibre |
| 5 | Apple | The ultimate grab-and-go keeper; lasts weeks without a fridge. | 🟡 snack, oats, slaw | 🟢 zero, just rinse | 🟢 | 🟢 fibre, polyphenols (quercetin), modest vit C |
| 6 | Strawberries | High vit C and no peeling; price swings with season. | 🟡 snack, yogurt, salad | 🟢 rinse, pull tops | 🟡 seasonal | 🟢 vit C, manganese, folate |
| 7 | Grapes | Effortless and crowd-pleasing, but lighter on standout nutrients. | 🟡 snack, cheese | 🟢 rinse | 🟢 | 🟡 vit K, polyphenols |

**Notes:**
- Frozen mixed berries are the smartest berry buy — same nutrition as fresh, 🟢 year-round, no spoilage. They cover fresh blueberries too.
- Bananas, apples, oranges and avocados are reliably 🟢 across the UK, the West and East/SE Asia; strawberries swing 🟡 on season.
- Honourable mention: pears — fibre, zero-prep, near-identical to apple and very common.
