# Proven — Bread & Wraps

No-cook carb vehicles for eggs, beans, fish and avocado. Other options considered
are in `../candidates/bread-and-wraps.md`.

**Dominant criterion: convenience + versatility as a no-cook vehicle.** Nutrition is
the tie-breaker — always reach for the wholemeal/seeded/wholegrain version (same
effort, more fibre and protein).

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wholemeal / seeded sliced bread | The default no-cook vehicle — takes every topping cold or toasted, and seeded/wholegrain wins the nutrition tie-break outright. | 🟢 sandwiches, toast, everything | 🟢 no cook; toast optional | 🟢 universal | 🟢 fibre, protein, B vits; seeds add zinc + some omega-3 |
| 2 | Wholemeal wrap / flour tortilla | Most versatile roll there is — fills and folds straight from the pack. | 🟢 wraps, burritos, roll-ups | 🟢 no cook; fill and roll | 🟢 near-universal | 🟡 fibre if wholemeal, else refined |
| 3 | Wholemeal pita | Built-in pocket holds eggs, beans or tuna without a plate; wholegrain and a staple. | 🟢 pockets, dippers, mini-pizza | 🟢 no cook; 20s warm to open | 🟡 UK/Med staple, patchier in Asia | 🟢 fibre, protein, manganese |

**Notes:**
- Always buy the wholemeal/seeded/wholegrain version — same effort, more fibre and protein.
- All three are genuinely no-cook; warming pita ~20s just opens the pocket.
