# Proven — Oils & Fats

The two oils worth keeping. Other options considered are in
`../candidates/oils-and-fats.md`.

**Dominant criterion: health + availability.** Taste barely separates oils — any
decent one is a neutral carrier — so these are picked on being healthy *and*
everywhere, with effort and versatility as tie-breakers.

## 1. Extra virgin olive oil — the everyday default
- **Why proven:** Best health evidence of any common oil (monounsaturated fat +
  polyphenols + vit E — the combination behind olive oil's cardiovascular track
  record). Near-universal stock across the UK, the West and Asia. Good raw
  (dressings, finishing) and at the medium-heat sautéing these recipes use.
- **Use for:** almost everything — sautéing greens and beans, dressings, finishing.

## 2. Rapeseed / canola — the hot-frying partner
- **Why proven:** Matches olive oil on shelf presence, and beats it on saturated
  fat (~7% vs ~14%) and omega-3 (~10×), with a higher smoke point — so it's the
  one to reach for when frying hot. Cheap and neutral.
- **Use for:** high-heat frying, and anything where olive flavour would intrude.

**Verdict:** no single oil is *both* as available *and* as healthy as olive oil,
so it stays the everyday default. Rapeseed complements rather than replaces it —
EVOO for raw/finishing and flavour, rapeseed for neutral high heat. Two bottles,
no compromise.
