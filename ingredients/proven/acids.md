# Proven — Acids

The brightening lever (*Salt Fat Acid Heat*). Other options considered are in
`../candidates/acids.md`.

**Dominant criterion: brightness + shelf life.** Acids are a flavour tool, not a
nutrition play — picked for the brightness they add and for covering both the fresh
and keeps-forever ends with as few bottles as possible.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Fresh lemon | Brightest all-rounder and the rare acid with real vit C; a whole fruit keeps 3–4 weeks in the fridge. | 🟢 dressings, fish, greens, soups, water | 🟢 halve + squeeze | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C, flavonoids |
| 2 | Red wine vinegar | The immortal cupboard acid — sharp, savoury and endlessly useful for dressings, lentils and deglazing. | 🟢 vinaigrettes, deglaze, marinades, lentils | 🟢 just pour | 🟢 | 🔴 negligible |

**Notes:**
- One perishable (lemon, weeks in the fridge) and one that keeps forever (vinegar) — covers fresh brightening and cupboard cooking both.
- Lime is the swap for Thai/Mexican food; white wine vinegar is the neutral stand-in for red. Both in `../candidates/acids.md`.
