# Proven — Nutrition Powerhouses

The nutrient-dense add-ons I keep. Other options considered are in
`../candidates/nutrition-powerhouses.md`.

**Dominant criterion: nutrition density + convenience.** These are stir-in add-ons
for a big nutrition hit, not cooking ingredients — picked on what they add per spoon
for near-zero effort.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ground flaxseed (milled) | Top nutrient-per-spoon add-on — huge plant omega-3 plus lignans, and it just stirs in. | 🟢 oats, yoghurt, soups, stir-in | 🟢 stir in (buy pre-milled) | 🟢 | 🟢 ALA omega-3, lignans, soluble fibre |
| 2 | Chia seeds | Same omega-3 league as flax with even more fibre and no grinding, and it gels into a no-cook pudding. | 🟢 pudding, oats, yoghurt, jam | 🟢 stir in or soak (passive) | 🟢 | 🟢 ALA omega-3, fibre, calcium |

**Notes:**
- Buy flax **pre-milled** — whole flax passes through undigested. Both flax and chia keep best cool; buy small.
- A spoon of each into oats or yoghurt is the easy daily habit; chia also sets into an overnight pudding.
- Both also appear under nuts & seeds — listed here as the concentrated add-on.
