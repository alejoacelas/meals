# Proven — Legumes & Beans

The pulses I keep. Other options considered are in
`../candidates/legumes-and-beans.md`.

**Dominant criterion: nutrition + no-soak convenience.** Legumes are the cheap
plant-protein-and-fibre core, so they're picked on nutrition and on cooking fast
with no soaking — the tin and the split lentil win.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Canned chickpeas | The most useful tin in the cupboard — ready in seconds and good in almost everything. | 🟢 curries, salads, pasta, pan-fried | 🟢 rinse, 0 cook | 🟢 | 🟢 protein, fibre, folate, iron |
| 2 | Red split lentils | The only dried legume worth it — cooks in one pot in 10–15 min with no soak, and thickens soups and sauces as it goes. | 🟢 dal, soup, sauce-thickener | 🟢 10–15 min, no soak | 🟢 | 🟢 protein, fibre, iron, folate |

**Notes:**
- Always rinse canned chickpeas to cut the sodium.
- Red split lentils collapse as they cook — they thicken a soup or sauce with no blender.
