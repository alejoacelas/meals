# Proven — Spices & Dried Herbs

> ⚠️ **Not yet reviewed.** All spices were moved here as a set rather than picked
> one by one — their nutrition barely matters (you use a pinch), and many do
> similar jobs. Which ones actually earn a permanent place gets decided from the
> recipes. For now: buy the set, learn to use them (`../notes/using-spices.md`),
> and we'll prune later.

**Dominant criterion: taste + versatility.** Spices are a flavour play, not a
nutrition one — ranked on how much they lift beans, greens and eggs for near-zero
effort. Every dish still works if one is missing.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ground cumin | The single biggest flavour-per-gram win for beans, so it leads. | 🟢 beans, chilli, curry, greens | 🟢 sprinkle/bloom 30s | 🟢 | 🟢 iron, manganese |
| 2 | Black pepper | Near-universal lift, and it boosts turmeric's curcumin absorption. | 🟢 everything | 🟢 grind/shake | 🟢 | 🟢 piperine, manganese |
| 3 | Chilli / red pepper flakes | Cheap heat that earns a place in beans, greens, pasta and stir-fries. | 🟢 heat for any savoury dish | 🟢 pinch in | 🟢 | 🟢 capsaicin, vit A |
| 4 | Garlic granules / powder | A flavour base with zero chopping and 🟢 everywhere. | 🟢 savoury base everywhere | 🟢 shake in | 🟢 | 🟢 trace minerals |
| 5 | Smoked paprika | Adds depth and smoke instantly; sweet paprika is the more universal swap. | 🟢 beans, stews, eggs, rice | 🟢 stir in | 🟡 sweet paprika 🟢 | 🟢 vit A, antioxidants |
| 6 | Ground turmeric | Colour and anti-inflammatory curcumin; pair with pepper to absorb it. | 🟢 curry, rice, greens, lentils | 🟢 bloom in oil | 🟢 | 🟢 curcumin, manganese, iron |
| 7 | Dried oregano | The Mediterranean workhorse for any tomato-and-bean pan. | 🟢 tomato, beans, greens | 🟢 crumble in | 🟢 | 🟢 vit K, antioxidants |
| 8 | Ground coriander | Cumin's other half; warm and citrusy, almost free of effort. | 🟢 partners cumin in curries | 🟢 sprinkle/bloom | 🟢 | 🟢 antioxidants |
| 9 | Curry powder | One scoop builds a whole flavour profile when you don't want to measure. | 🟢 instant one-jar curry | 🟢 bloom in oil | 🟢 | 🟢 turmeric + cumin blend |
| 10 | Ground cinnamon | Bridges breakfast oats and savoury bean stews from a single jar. | 🟢 sweet + savoury stews, porridge | 🟢 sprinkle | 🟢 | 🟢 manganese, antioxidants |
| 11 | Ground ginger | Warmth for Asian-leaning dishes without grating fresh root. | 🟡 stir-fries, curries, warm drinks | 🟢 shake in | 🟢 | 🟢 shogaols, antioxidants |
| 12 | Bay leaves | Lovely background depth, but only pays off in a pot you simmer a while. | 🟡 long-simmer bean/lentil pots | 🟢 drop in, fish out | 🟡 | 🟢 minimal per serving |

**Notes:**
- Nutrition is small for all spices — they earn their place on taste, versatility and near-zero effort.
- Black pepper's piperine sharply raises turmeric's curcumin absorption — use them together.
- Availability — buy smoked paprika in the West (sweet paprika is the 🟢 swap in Asia); check curry-powder blends for added salt; European bay leaves are 🟡 in East/SE-Asian shops (which carry a different "Indian bay leaf").
- Also worth grabbing: garam masala (richer than curry powder), whole mustard seeds and cayenne (narrower use), dried mixed/Italian herbs (a fine oregano stand-in).
