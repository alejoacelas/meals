# Proven — Vegetables

The everyday vegetables. Other options considered are in
`../candidates/vegetables.md`. Onion and garlic are vegetables too, but they live
with the aromatic base in `aromatics.md`.

**Dominant criterion: nutrition + versatility.** Vegetables are the nutritional
core of these meals, so they're picked on nutrient density and how many pans they
go in — and all of these also cook fast with just a knife and a pan.

| Rank | Ingredient | Why here | Versatility | Prep effort | Availability | Nutrition |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spinach | One of the most nutrient-dense greens, and it cooks instantly in any pan. | 🟢 | 🟢 wilts in 1 min | 🟢 | 🟢 vit K, vit A, folate, magnesium |
| 2 | Tomato | Eat raw or collapse into a quick sauce; tinned backup year-round. | 🟢 raw or sauce | 🟢 chop, no cook needed | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C, potassium, lycopene |
| 3 | Mushroom | Meaty umami booster that browns fast in an oiled pan. | 🟢 umami | 🟢 slice, 5 min sear | 🟢 | 🟡 B vitamins, selenium, copper |
| 4 | Bell pepper | Very high vit C (red especially), sweet; great raw or quick-fried. | 🟢 raw or fried | 🟢 slice, 4–5 min | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C, vit A |
| 5 | Broccoli | Top-tier brassica nutrition; stir-fry small florets, just needs cutting. | 🟢 | 🟡 cut small, 5–6 min | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C, vit K, folate, sulforaphane |
| 6 | Carrot | Big vit A and long-keeping, but slice thin or it cooks slowly. | 🟢 raw or cooked | 🟡 thin slices to cook fast | 🟢 | 🟢 vit A/beta-carotene, fibre |
| 7 | Cabbage | Cheap, keeps for weeks, shreds and stir-fries fast. | 🟢 | 🟢 shred, 5 min stir-fry | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C, vit K, fibre |
| 8 | Kale | Nutrition on par with spinach (more vit C, slightly less vit K), but tougher and slower to cook. | 🟢 | 🟡 strip stems, 5+ min | 🟢 | 🟢 vit C, vit K, vit A |
| 9 | Pak choi | Stir-fry star that wilts fast; calcium is low-oxalate so well absorbed. | 🟢 stir-fry | 🟢 halve, 3–4 min | 🟡 | 🟢 vit C, vit A, vit K, calcium |

**Notes:**
- Pak choi is 🟢 across the UK and East/SE Asia but more of an international veg elsewhere.
- Frozen spinach and frozen peas are great zero-prep, long-keeping backups.
- Pair spinach with a vit-C veg here (tomato, pepper) to absorb more of its iron.
