## Chickpea & Egg Deli Salad
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~10m · 1 session · ~15m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟡
**Equipment:** one pot, knife
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | canned chickpeas, eggs, tomato, onion, extra-virgin olive oil, red wine vinegar, fresh lemon, chilli flakes, fine sea salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~10m · ~15m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | boil the eggs ~9 min while you work → quick-pickle sliced onion in vinegar and salt → chop tomato, drain chickpeas → peel and chop the eggs → fold everything with oil, lemon, chilli, salt and pepper |

A room-temperature deli salad with double protein: creamy egg and chickpeas
brightened by tomato and vinegar-quick-pickled onion. The only heat you need is a
pot of boiling water, so it goes anywhere.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 2–3 eggs
- 1 tomato, chopped (or a handful of tinned / cherry tomatoes)
- ½ onion, thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- ½ lemon
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Extra-virgin olive oil, fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Lower the eggs into a pot of boiling water and simmer 9 min for just-set yolks. Lift out and run under cold water until cool enough to peel.
2. While they cook, toss the sliced onion with the vinegar and a pinch of salt in your serving bowl — leave it to soften and turn pink.
3. Chop the tomato and tip in with the drained chickpeas. Add a good glug of olive oil, a squeeze of lemon, the chilli flakes, salt and plenty of black pepper.
4. Peel and roughly chop the eggs, fold them through gently, and check the seasoning. Eat warm or at room temp.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Salty protein boost (+ feta or tinned tuna, not in kit):** crumble feta or flake a drained tin of tuna through for brine, creaminess and even more protein.
- **Deli freshness (+ fresh parsley or dill, not in kit):** a good handful chopped in lifts it to a proper deli-counter salad.
- **Sharper dressing (+ wholegrain mustard, not in kit):** whisk a teaspoon into the oil and vinegar before folding for a tangy, clinging dressing.
- **Creamy body (+ avocado, not in kit):** cube and fold in at the end for buttery richness and healthy fat.

**Why it scores well:** Dominant criterion is **availability** — a tin of chickpeas, a few eggs and one onion are everywhere, and a single pot of boiling water is the only equipment. Chickpeas plus eggs stack plant and complete protein with fibre, B12, choline and iron (nutrition 🟢); it's one continuous session with no real cooking (effort 🟢). Core taste is good but plain — the deli-salad sparkle lives in the level-ups (taste 🟡).
