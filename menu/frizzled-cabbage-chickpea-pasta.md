## Frizzled Cabbage & Chickpea Pasta
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟡 (active ~15m · 1 session · ~25m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pot, one pan, knife
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | pasta (fusilli/penne), cabbage, canned chickpeas, onion, garlic, ground cumin, chilli flakes, fresh lemon, extra-virgin olive oil, fine sea salt |
| **Time** | active ~15m · ~25m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | boil pasta in salted water → fry shredded cabbage and onion hard in olive oil until the edges frizzle → add garlic, cumin, chilli and chickpeas to crisp → toss with drained pasta, a splash of pasta water, lemon and salt |

Cabbage fried hard until the edges go sweet, charred and nutty, with cumin-warmed
chickpeas folded through pasta. Cheap, long-keeping veg that eats like much more
than it cost.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 150 g pasta (fusilli or penne)
- ¼ cabbage, shredded (a few big handfuls)
- 1 can chickpeas, drained and patted dry
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- ½ lemon
- Extra-virgin olive oil, fine sea salt

**Method**
1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water. Scoop out a mugful of the water before draining.
2. Meanwhile, heat a generous glug of olive oil in the pan over high heat. Add the cabbage and onion and fry hard, stirring only now and then, until the edges char and frizzle — 8–10 min.
3. Push to one side; add the chickpeas, garlic, cumin and chilli and let the chickpeas crisp and the garlic soften, ~3 min. Salt to taste.
4. Toss in the drained pasta with a splash of the pasta water to loosen. Squeeze over the lemon, check the salt, and serve.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Salty creamy finish (+ feta, not in kit):** crumble over off the heat — its sharp tang cuts the sweet cabbage.
- **Deeper char (+ smoked paprika, not in kit):** a teaspoon in with the cumin adds smoke that doubles down on the frizzle.
- **Crunch (+ pumpkin or sunflower seeds, not in kit):** toast dry in the pan first and scatter on top for texture and extra healthy fat.
- **Earthier swap (+ tinned green lentils, not in kit):** use in place of chickpeas for a softer, more savoury bite.

**Why it scores well:** Dominant criterion is **availability** — cabbage, an onion, a tin of chickpeas and dry pasta are everywhere and keep for weeks. Chickpeas and cabbage carry real protein, fibre and vitamin C/K (nutrition 🟢); it's one pot, one pan, one session (effort 🟡 only because the hard frizzle wants tending). Frizzling is what makes humble cabbage genuinely tasty.
