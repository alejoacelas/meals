## Smoky Cumin Chickpea Chilli
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟡 (active ~12m · 1 session · ~30m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pot / knife
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | canned chickpeas, onion, bell pepper, tinned chopped tomato, garlic, ground cumin, chilli flakes, extra-virgin olive oil, red wine vinegar, salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~12m · ~30m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | hard-brown the onion in oil → soften pepper, then bloom garlic, cumin, chilli → add tomato + chickpeas, mash a few, simmer thick → sharpen with red wine vinegar, salt, pepper |

A thick one-pot chilli where bloomed cumin and a hard-browned onion stand in for a whole spice rack — and it only gets better reheated the next day.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 can chopped tomatoes (~400g)
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1½ tsp ground cumin
- chilli flakes, to taste
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp red wine vinegar
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Warm a good glug of olive oil in the pot over medium-high. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook, stirring now and then, until deep golden-brown at the edges — 8–10 min. This browning is most of the flavour, so let it go further than feels comfortable.
2. Stir in the bell pepper and cook 3–4 min until it slumps.
3. Drop the heat, add the garlic, cumin and chilli flakes, and toss ~1 min until fragrant and the cumin smells toasty.
4. Pour in the tomatoes and chickpeas, season with salt and pepper, and bring to a simmer. Mash a handful of the chickpeas against the side of the pot to thicken. Simmer, stirring occasionally, 12–15 min, until thick enough to hold a spoon-trail.
5. Off the heat, stir in the red wine vinegar and taste for salt and heat. Serve, or cool and reheat tomorrow when it's even better.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Deeper smoke (+ smoked paprika, not in kit):** a teaspoon with the cumin pushes it from chilli-flake heat toward proper barbecue depth.
- **Bulk it out (+ kidney or black beans, not in kit):** tip in a drained can alongside the chickpeas for a heartier, two-bean chilli that stretches to more servings.
- **Sweetness and bite (+ sweetcorn, not in kit):** a handful stirred in for the last few minutes adds pops of sweetness against the smoky base.
- **Make it a plate (+ rice, corn tortillas, avocado, coriander or lime, not in kit):** spoon over rice or roll into tortillas, then finish with sliced avocado, coriander and a squeeze of lime; (+ grated cheddar or feta, not in kit) melts in for a richer bowl.

**Why it scores well:** Taste leads — a hard-browned onion plus bloomed cumin builds a smoky, savoury base that tastes slow-cooked from two spices, and reheating only deepens it. It's also genuinely nutrient-dense (chickpea protein and fibre, vit-C-rich pepper and tomato, olive oil for healthy fat), every item is an ordinary shelf staple, and it's one pot in one continuous session.
