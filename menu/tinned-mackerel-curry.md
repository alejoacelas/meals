## Tinned Mackerel Curry

**Equipment:** one pan / knife
**Serves:** 1–2

A fast South-Indian-style fish curry: a spiced turmeric-tomato base built in one pan, with tinned mackerel folded in at the very end so it stays in tender flakes, brightened by lemon. It's the catalog's first fish dish — omega-3, B12 and vitamin D in a store-cupboard bowl ready in about 20 minutes.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1–2 tins mackerel (~125g each), drained
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 can chopped tomatoes (~400g)
- 1 tsp curry powder
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- chilli flakes, to taste
- 2 handfuls spinach
- Rapeseed/canola oil
- ½ lemon
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Warm a good glug of oil in the pan over medium. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook, stirring now and then, until soft and lightly golden — 6–8 min.
2. Drop the heat, add the garlic and all the spices (curry powder, turmeric, ginger, coriander, cumin, chilli flakes), and toss ~1 min until fragrant and the kitchen smells toasty.
3. Pour in the tomatoes with a splash of water, season with salt and pepper, and simmer, stirring occasionally, 6–8 min — until thick, jammy and no longer raw-tasting.
4. Stir in the spinach and let it wilt down, ~1 min.
5. Off the heat, add the mackerel in big flakes and fold gently so it stays in tender pieces rather than breaking up. Warm through for a minute on a low flame.
6. Squeeze over the lemon and taste for salt, pepper and heat. Serve as is, or over rice.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Sour South-Indian edge (+ tamarind, not in kit):** a teaspoon of tamarind paste stirred into the base (or extra lime) gives the tangy backbone a proper fish curry wants.
- **Kerala-creamy (+ coconut milk, not in kit):** swap the splash of water for half a tin of coconut milk and simmer to a silky, mellow gravy that softens the chilli.
- **Tempered oil (+ mustard seeds + curry leaves, not in kit):** before the onion, crackle a teaspoon of black mustard seeds and a sprig of curry leaves in the hot oil for a South-Indian aroma you can't fake.
- **Make it a plate (+ rice + fresh coriander, not in kit):** spoon over rice and scatter with coriander; or swap the mackerel for tinned sardines for a softer, richer fish.

**Why it scores well:** Nutrition leads, and hard — oily mackerel brings omega-3, B12 and vitamin D that nothing else in the kit offers, plus complete protein, with iron and folate from the spinach and vitamin C from the tomato. It's also one pan in a single ~20-minute session, every item is an ordinary shelf staple, and folding the fish in off the heat keeps it in tender, genuinely tasty flakes.
