## Lemon-Dressed Chickpea & Raw Veg Bowl
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~8m · 1 session · ~8m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟡
**Equipment:** knife (and a bowl)
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | canned chickpeas, tomato, bell pepper, carrot, onion, fresh lemon, extra-virgin olive oil, red wine vinegar, ground cumin, chilli flakes, fine sea salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~8m · ~8m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | drain the chickpeas → macerate sliced onion in lemon, vinegar and salt → dice tomato and pepper, grate carrot → whisk oil, cumin and chilli into the lemon to make a dressing → fold everything together and season |

The flagship no-cook bowl: a tin of chickpeas, a handful of raw veg, and a sharp lemon-cumin dressing tossed in one bowl. Bright, crunchy, high-protein, and on the table in eight minutes without turning on the stove.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- 1 tomato, diced (or a handful of cherry / tinned tomatoes)
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1 carrot, coarsely grated
- ½ onion, thinly sliced
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- ½ tsp ground cumin
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Extra-virgin olive oil, fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Tip the drained chickpeas into your serving bowl. Slice the onion thinly and toss it with the lemon juice, vinegar and a pinch of salt — leave it to soften while you chop (this takes the raw bite off and turns it pink).
2. Dice the tomato and pepper small, and coarsely grate the carrot, so every forkful catches a bit of everything.
3. Whisk a good glug of olive oil into the onion-lemon mix with the cumin, chilli flakes, salt and plenty of black pepper to make a loose dressing.
4. Add all the veg to the chickpeas, pour over the dressing and fold well. Check the salt and acid — it's even better after a 5-minute rest, but good straight away.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Salty protein boost (+ feta or tinned tuna, not in kit):** crumble feta or flake a drained tin of tuna through for brine, creaminess and even more protein.
- **Creamy richness (+ avocado, not in kit):** cube and fold in at the end for buttery healthy fat that softens the raw crunch.
- **Fresh lift (+ parsley or coriander, not in kit):** a big handful chopped through turns it from salad into something herby and alive.
- **Nutty crunch (+ toasted sunflower or pumpkin seeds, not in kit):** scatter over for texture, magnesium and zinc — toast them in a dry pan first if you have a minute.

**Why it scores well:** Dominant criterion is **nutrition** — chickpeas bring plant protein and fibre, while the raw tomato, pepper, carrot and lemon pile on vitamin C, vitamin A and folate, with the vit C helping you absorb the chickpeas' iron; olive oil adds the healthy fat (nutrition 🟢). It's the lowest-effort dish in the kit — no heat, no pan, one continuous session of chopping (effort 🟢), and every ingredient is a supermarket basic (availability 🟢). The core is genuinely bright and crunchy but a touch austere on its own; the feta, avocado and herbs are where it gets crave-able (taste 🟡).
