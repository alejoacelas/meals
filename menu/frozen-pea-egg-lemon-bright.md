## Frozen Pea & Egg, Lemon-Bright
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~7m · 1 session · ~12m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pan / knife
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | frozen peas, eggs, garlic, extra-virgin olive oil, lemon, chilli flakes, fine sea salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~7m · ~12m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | bloom a smashed garlic clove in oil → simmer frozen peas in a splash of water → fold in beaten eggs to a soft scramble → off heat: lemon, chilli, pepper, a final drizzle of oil |

Sweet peas straight from the freezer, soft eggs and a hit of lemon — bright, satisfying, and not a single thing to chop. The whole dish leans on store-cupboard and freezer staples, so it's the one to make when you've got five spare minutes and zero patience.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 150g frozen peas (about 2 generous handfuls)
- 3–4 eggs, beaten with a pinch of salt
- 1 clove garlic, smashed flat with the side of the knife (no chopping)
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ lemon (a little zest if you have it, plus the juice)
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Warm a good glug of olive oil over medium heat; add the smashed garlic and let it perfume the oil ~1 min, then lift it out (or leave it — your call).
2. Tip in the frozen peas with a splash of water; cook, stirring now and then, until bright green and tender, ~3–4 min. Season lightly.
3. Drop the heat to low. Pour in the beaten eggs and stir gently, folding through the peas, until just softly set — ~1–2 min. Don't overcook; they finish off the heat.
4. Off the heat, squeeze over the lemon (and a little zest), scatter chilli flakes and plenty of black pepper, and finish with a thread of olive oil.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Salty crumble (+ feta or shaved parmesan, not in kit):** scatter over the warm eggs so it half-melts into them for a savoury anchor.
- **Fresh lift (+ torn mint, not in kit):** mint and peas are a classic; tear a few leaves over at the end for a cool, sweet edge.
- **Creamy spoon (+ Greek yoghurt or crème fraîche, not in kit):** a dollop alongside turns it richer and more breakfast-like, and tames the chilli.
- **Make it a full plate (+ toast, or flaked hot-smoked salmon / sliced avocado, not in kit):** pile onto hot toast, or fold through flaked salmon or avocado for protein and staying power.

**Why it scores well:** Effort leads — no board, no chopping, one pan, one continuous session, peas straight from the freezer. Nutrition keeps pace: complete protein and healthy fat from the eggs and oil, fibre, plant protein and vitamin C from the peas, micronutrients from the lemon. Every core item is an ordinary supermarket staple, and sweet peas against soft egg, bright lemon and a little heat make it genuinely good to eat.
