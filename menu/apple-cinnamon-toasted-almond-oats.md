## Apple-Cinnamon & Toasted Almond Oats
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~8m · 1 session · ~15m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pot / knife (a box grater helps but isn't essential)
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | rolled oats, water, apple, ground cinnamon, ground ginger, almonds, Greek yoghurt, fine sea salt |
| **Time** | active ~8m · ~15m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | toast the almonds dry in the pot, tip out → grate in the apple with oats, water, cinnamon, ginger & a pinch of salt, simmer creamy → top with cool Greek yoghurt and the almonds |

Apple-pie porridge: a whole apple grated straight into the oats half-melts into sweetness, warm cinnamon and a little ginger do the work of a spice rack, and cool Greek yoghurt plus dry-toasted almonds finish it with tang and crunch. The showcase for apple and almonds — taste-led, with no added sugar needed.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- A small handful (~25g) almonds, roughly chopped
- 50g (½ cup) rolled oats
- ~300ml water
- 1 apple, coarsely grated (skin on; core left behind)
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- ¼ tsp ground ginger
- A pinch of fine sea salt
- 2–3 tbsp Greek yoghurt, to finish

**Method**
1. Put the pot on medium heat and toast the chopped almonds dry, tossing, 3–4 min until golden and fragrant. Tip them out onto a plate.
2. Into the same pot add the oats, water, cinnamon, ginger and salt. Grate the apple straight in (stop at the core) and stir.
3. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook, stirring now and then, 6–8 min until creamy and the apple has half-melted into the oats. Add a splash more water if it tightens too far.
4. Spoon into a bowl, top with a cool dollop of Greek yoghurt, and scatter over the toasted almonds. Eat hot.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Jammy sweetness (+ raisins or sultanas, not in kit):** stir a small handful in with the oats — they plump as they cook and melt into pockets of caramel sweetness.
- **Drizzle to finish (+ tahini or honey, not in kit):** a thread of tahini over the yoghurt adds nutty richness, or honey if you want it properly dessert-like.
- **Browner, nuttier almonds (+ butter, not in kit):** toast the almonds in a small knob of butter instead of dry for a brown-butter note that lifts the whole bowl.
- **Omega-3 boost (+ ground flaxseed, not in kit):** stir a teaspoon into the finished oats for extra fibre and healthy fats without changing the flavour.

**Why it scores well:** Taste leads — grated apple gives natural sweetness while cinnamon and ginger carry warmth, and the yoghurt-and-almond finish adds contrast in tang and crunch. Nutrition holds up: oat fibre, almond fats and Greek-yoghurt protein make it a balanced breakfast, not just carbs. And it's one pot, one continuous session, ~15 minutes start to bowl.
