## Mackerel & Oat Fishcakes, Smoky Paprika
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟡 (active ~15m · 1 session · ~25m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pan / knife / bowl
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | tinned mackerel, rolled oats, eggs, onion, garlic granules, smoked paprika, frozen peas, lemon, rapeseed/canola oil, salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~15m · ~25m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | mash mackerel with beaten egg, onion, garlic granules, smoked paprika & oats → rest 5 min so the oats drink up the moisture → form patties → pan-fry both sides to a crisp golden crust → simmer peas in the same pan → plate with lemon |

Fishcakes with no potato to boil and no oven — rolled oats are the binder, soaking up the oil and moisture before crisping to a savoury, golden crust. A no-waste way to make a tin feel like a proper cooked meal, and the mix keeps and fries in batches.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 2 tins mackerel (~125g each), drained well
- 1 egg, beaten
- ½ onion, very finely diced
- ½ tsp garlic granules
- 1 tsp smoked (or sweet) paprika
- 40g (~½ cup) rolled oats
- A handful (~75g) frozen peas
- ½ lemon
- Rapeseed/canola oil, for frying
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Tip the drained mackerel into a bowl and mash with a fork. Add the beaten egg, onion, garlic granules, paprika and oats, with a squeeze of lemon, salt and a good grind of pepper. Mix to an even, slightly sticky mash.
2. Let it sit 5 minutes — the oats swell and drink up the moisture, firming the mix so it holds. With damp hands, press into 4 patties about 1.5cm thick.
3. Heat a thin layer of oil in the pan over medium. Fry the patties, undisturbed, 3–4 min a side until deep golden and crisp — flip once, gently. Lift onto a plate.
4. Tip the frozen peas into the same pan with a splash of water, simmer 2–3 min until bright and hot, season, and lightly crush a few with the fork.
5. Spoon the peas alongside, set the fishcakes on top, and finish with the rest of the lemon squeezed over.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Sauce it (+ Greek yoghurt, in kit; + Dijon, not in kit):** whip Greek yoghurt with lemon and a little salt for a quick dip — or stir in Dijon for a tartare-ish tang.
- **Fresh aromatics (+ spring onion + fresh coriander, not in kit):** fold sliced spring onion and chopped coriander through the mix for lift and colour.
- **Sweet-sharp glaze (+ sweet chilli or sriracha, not in kit):** brush a little over the patties in the last minute so it catches and caramelises.
- **Crunchier crust (+ panko breadcrumbs, not in kit):** press the formed patties into panko before frying for an extra-shattering coat.

**Why it scores well:** Nutrition leads — oily mackerel brings omega-3, B12 and vitamin D, the egg and peas add complete protein and fibre, and the oat binder folds in beta-glucan instead of plain starch. It's one pan in a single session from ordinary shelf staples; the only ding is that forming and flipping patties is a touch more hands-on than a stir-together bowl, and the smoky-paprika crust with lemon makes it genuinely good to eat.
