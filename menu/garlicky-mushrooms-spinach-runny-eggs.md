## Garlicky Mushrooms & Spinach with Runny Eggs
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~12m · 1 session · ~18m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pan, knife
**Serves:** 1–2

|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | mushroom, garlic, spinach, extra-virgin olive oil, eggs, fine sea salt, black pepper, chilli flakes, fresh lemon |
| **Time** | active ~12m · ~18m total · 1 session |

The kale-mushroom sauté grown up: hard-seared umami mushrooms and wilted spinach under runny yolks, now a complete egg-protein meal in one pan. Taste leads — everything else comes free.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 2–3 handfuls mushrooms, sliced thick
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 2 handfuls spinach (fresh or frozen)
- 2–3 eggs
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Fine sea salt, black pepper
- A squeeze of fresh lemon

**Method**
1. Heat a good glug of olive oil in the pan over medium-high. Add the mushrooms in one layer and leave them undisturbed 3–4 min, then turn and sear the other side until deep golden and shrunken — don't crowd or rush them; the hard sear is the whole flavour. Season with salt.
2. Drop the heat a little. Add the garlic and chilli flakes; cook 30–60 sec until fragrant.
3. Pile in the spinach and stir until just wilted — a minute for fresh, a little longer for frozen (let the water cook off).
4. Spread the veg out and make 2–3 wells. Crack an egg into each, season the whites, and cover with a lid or plate (or spoon the hot oil over them) until the whites set but the yolks stay runny, 2–4 min.
5. Off the heat, squeeze over lemon and grind on black pepper. Eat straight from the pan.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Toast underneath (+ bread, not in kit):** pile everything onto a slice of toast so the runny yolk and garlicky oil soak in — turns it into a fuller meal.
- **Deeper umami (+ soy sauce, not in kit):** splash a teaspoon over the mushrooms as they finish searing to amplify the savoury hit.
- **Make it heartier (+ tinned cannellini or white beans, not in kit):** warm a drained handful with the mushrooms for extra protein and fibre.
- **Savoury finish (+ grated parmesan, not in kit):** shower over at the end, or swirl in a knob of butter (not in kit) for a glossier, richer pan.

**Why it scores well:** Taste is the dominant criterion and it delivers — a hard sear concentrates the mushrooms' umami and the runny yolk becomes a sauce, kept bright by chilli and lemon. Underneath that it's genuinely complete: egg protein, mushroom and spinach micronutrients, healthy fat from the oil — all in one pan, one continuous session, from nine everyday ingredients.
