## Aglio e Olio with Greens & Chickpeas
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟡 (active ~12m · 1 session · ~20m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pot, one pan, knife
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | pasta (fusilli/penne), garlic, extra-virgin olive oil, chilli flakes, fresh lemon, broccoli, canned chickpeas, fine sea salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~12m · ~20m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | boil pasta + broccoli (save a mug of water) → sizzle garlic & chilli in olive oil, warm chickpeas → toss in pasta, broccoli & a splash of water to emulsify → salt, pepper, lemon |

The Roman classic, made a full meal: garlic, oil, chilli and lemon whipped with starchy pasta water into a glossy sauce, with chickpeas and broccoli carrying the protein, fibre and bite. Taste-led, and the payoff is well above the effort.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- ~120 g fusilli or penne
- 1 small head broccoli, cut into small florets
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 3–4 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- A generous glug extra-virgin olive oil (3–4 tbsp)
- A good pinch chilli flakes
- ½ lemon
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water. Drop the broccoli florets into the same pot for the last 3–4 minutes. Before draining, scoop out a mug of the starchy water.
2. Meanwhile, warm the olive oil in a cold pan with the garlic and chilli over low–medium heat. Let them sizzle gently to pale gold and fragrant — don't let the garlic brown. Add the chickpeas and warm through, pressing a few against the pan so they catch a little.
3. Tip the drained pasta and broccoli into the pan with a good splash of pasta water. Toss hard for a minute so the oil and water emulsify into a glossy sauce that clings — add more pasta water if it looks oily.
4. Off the heat, season with salt and plenty of black pepper, then squeeze over the lemon and toss once more.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Cheesy & sharp (+ parmesan, not in kit):** a handful grated in off the heat tightens the emulsion and adds savoury depth.
- **Crunch (+ toasted breadcrumbs or chopped almonds, not in kit):** toast dry in the pan first, scatter over at the end for contrast against the soft pasta.
- **Fresh lift (+ flat-leaf parsley, not in kit):** a chopped handful stirred through brightens the whole bowl.
- **Umami bomb (+ anchovy, not in kit):** melt one fillet into the garlic oil until it dissolves — it seasons everything; or swap the chickpeas for a tin of tuna (not in kit).

**Why it scores well:** Taste is the point and it delivers — the garlic-chilli-lemon emulsion is genuinely excellent, and chickpeas plus broccoli turn it into a complete, nutrient-dense meal (plant protein, fibre, healthy fat). One continuous session, pot/pan/knife only, every ingredient on a normal shelf. The half-point on effort is just the careful garlic step and the toss to emulsify.
