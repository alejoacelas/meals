## Spiced Red Lentil Soup, Harira-Style
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟡 (active ~12m · 1 session · ~40m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟡
**Equipment:** one pot / knife
**Serves:** 2–3 (made to batch)

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | red split lentils, onion, carrot, garlic, tinned chopped tomato, ground cumin, ground coriander, ground cinnamon, chilli flakes, extra-virgin olive oil, fresh lemon, fine sea salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~12m · ~40m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | sweat onion + carrot in oil → toast garlic, cumin, coriander, cinnamon, chilli → cook down tinned tomato → add rinsed lentils + water, simmer ~25m until self-thickened → finish with lemon, salt, pepper |

A brothy, batchable lentil soup that leans North African — cumin and coriander with a quiet pinch of cinnamon, tomato and carrot for body, lemon to finish. Red lentils dissolve into the broth and thicken it on their own, so there's no blender and no fuss — just a fibre-and-protein bowl that reheats all week.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 150 g red split lentils, rinsed
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced small (~1 cm)
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 can chopped tomatoes (~400 g)
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ¼ tsp ground cinnamon
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- ~1.2 L water
- ½ lemon
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Rinse the lentils in a sieve until the water runs clear; set aside. Warm a good glug of olive oil in the pot and sweat the onion and carrot over medium heat, 6–8 min, until softening and sweet.
2. Stir in the garlic, cumin, coriander, cinnamon and chilli flakes; toast ~1 min until fragrant — the cinnamon should read as warmth, not pudding.
3. Add the chopped tomatoes and a pinch of salt; cook down 2–3 min until jammy and a shade darker.
4. Tip in the lentils and the water. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring now and then so they don't catch, ~20–25 min, until the lentils collapse and the broth thickens to a loose, spoonable soup. Loosen with a splash more water if it tightens.
5. Off the heat, squeeze in the lemon and season hard with salt and black pepper. Taste, and push the cumin or chilli until the bowl reads warm and bright rather than flat.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Savoury backbone (+ stock cube, not in kit):** crumble one into the water in step 4 — the single biggest upgrade, turning plain broth into proper soup.
- **Fuller, true harira (+ a tin of chickpeas, in kit):** add a drained tin with the lentils for extra heft and a more authentic, stew-like body.
- **Fresh + creamy finish (+ fresh coriander or parsley, not in kit; + Greek yoghurt, in kit):** scatter torn herbs over each bowl and swirl in a spoon of yoghurt for cool, tangy contrast and a little more protein.
- **Heat and smoke (+ harissa, not in kit):** a spoonful stirred in at the end brings North African chilli warmth and a smoky depth behind the cinnamon.

**Why it scores well:** Nutrition leads — red lentils stack plant protein and fibre with iron and folate, carrots load it with beta-carotene, tomato adds vitamin C and lycopene, and olive oil the healthy fat, with nothing empty in the pot. It's all ordinary shelf staples in one pot (availability 🟢) over one unbroken session, though the ~25-minute simmer keeps effort at 🟡. The warm-spice trio and a hard squeeze of lemon make the water-based core genuinely good; a stock cube or a swirl of yoghurt takes it from solid to great (taste 🟡).
