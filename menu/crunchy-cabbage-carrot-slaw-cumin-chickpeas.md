## Crunchy Cabbage & Carrot Slaw with Cumin Chickpeas
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~10m · 1 session · ~10m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟡
**Equipment:** knife (no stove needed)
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | cabbage, carrot, onion, canned chickpeas, fresh lemon, red wine vinegar, extra-virgin olive oil, ground cumin, chilli flakes, fine sea salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~10m · ~10m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | whisk a cumin–lemon–vinegar dressing in the bowl and steep the sliced onion in it → shred cabbage and grate carrot → fold in drained chickpeas and toss → eat now or let it sit and keep for days |

A no-cook slaw you shred once and live off for days: cabbage and carrot stay crunchy, cumin and chilli warm the dressing, and chickpeas turn it into a real meal. It only gets better as it sits in the fridge.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- ¼ small cabbage, finely shredded (a few big handfuls)
- 1 large carrot, grated or cut into thin matchsticks
- ½ onion, thinly sliced
- 1 can chickpeas, drained and rinsed
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. In your serving bowl, whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, vinegar, cumin, chilli flakes, a good pinch of salt and plenty of black pepper. Stir the sliced onion in and leave it to mellow while you do the rest.
2. Finely shred the cabbage and grate (or matchstick) the carrot — the thinner you go, the better they soften and drink up the dressing.
3. Tip the cabbage, carrot and drained chickpeas into the bowl and toss well so everything is coated. Taste and adjust salt and lemon.
4. Eat straight away, or leave it 15–30 min (or chill overnight) — it improves as the veg softens and the flavours meld. Keeps 3–4 days in the fridge.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Creamy coleslaw turn (+ Greek yoghurt, not in kit):** stir a couple of spoonfuls into the dressing for a tangy, creamy slaw and a protein bump.
- **Satay lean (+ peanut butter, not in kit; + lime, not in kit):** whisk a spoon of peanut butter and a squeeze of lime into the dressing for a nutty, slaw-noodle-style hit.
- **Crunch & good fat (+ toasted peanuts or sunflower seeds, not in kit):** scatter a handful over the top for texture and healthy fat.
- **Fresh & sweet (+ fresh coriander, not in kit; + grated apple or sultanas, not in kit):** a handful of chopped coriander and some grated apple or a few sultanas lift and round it out.

**Why it scores well:** Dominant criterion is **low effort** — no stove, no cooking, one knife and one bowl, done in about 10 minutes and then it feeds you for days with zero further attention (effort 🟢). Chickpeas bring protein and fibre, cabbage and carrot pile on vitamin C, K and beta-carotene, and the olive oil adds healthy fat (nutrition 🟢); every ingredient is a supermarket staple (availability 🟢). The core is a genuinely good everyday slaw — punchy with cumin, chilli and onion — but it's plain until a creamy or nutty dressing and some fresh herbs take it up a level (taste 🟡).
