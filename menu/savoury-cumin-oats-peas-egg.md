## Savoury Cumin Oats with Peas & Egg
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~10m · 1 session · ~15m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟡
**Equipment:** one pan / knife
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | rolled oats, frozen peas, onion, garlic, ground cumin, chilli flakes, rapeseed oil, eggs, lemon, salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~10m · ~15m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | temper onion, garlic, cumin & chilli in oil → stir in oats, water & peas, simmer to creamy → fry the egg in the same pan → plate the oats, top with egg, lemon & a pinch more chilli |

An upma-inspired savoury porridge: oats tempered with cumin, onion and garlic, sweetened by peas, finished with a soft egg. The core-viable non-sweet breakfast — no sugar or fruit needed.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 tbsp rapeseed oil
- ½ small onion, finely diced
- 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- A pinch of chilli flakes (plus more to finish)
- 50g (½ cup) rolled oats
- A handful (~50g) frozen peas
- ~350ml water
- 1–2 eggs
- A squeeze of fresh lemon
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium. Soften the onion 3–4 min, then add the garlic, cumin and chilli flakes and fry ~1 min until fragrant.
2. Stir in the oats to toast for 30 sec, pour in the water and tip in the frozen peas. Simmer, stirring now and then, 4–5 min until creamy and the oats are tender. Season with salt and pepper, then scrape onto a plate.
3. Wipe the pan quickly if it's wet and add a touch more oil if needed, then crack the egg(s) straight into the hot pan and fry to your liking — cover for a soft-set top. Slide it onto the oats.
4. Squeeze over lemon, add a final pinch of chilli, and eat hot.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Authentic temper (+ mustard seeds, not in kit):** pop a teaspoon of black/brown mustard seeds in the hot oil until they crackle, before the onion — the defining note of a real upma.
- **Golden & warming (+ turmeric, not in kit; + fresh ginger, not in kit):** add ¼ tsp turmeric and a grated thumb of ginger with the cumin for colour, warmth and an anti-inflammatory edge.
- **Cool & fresh (+ Greek yoghurt, not in kit; + spring onion, not in kit; + fresh coriander, not in kit):** finish with a dollop of yoghurt and a scatter of sliced spring onion or torn coriander to lift the whole bowl.
- **Crunch & extra protein (+ roasted peanuts, not in kit):** scatter a small handful of chopped roasted peanuts over the top for texture and staying power.

**Why it scores well:** Low effort is the win — one pan, one continuous session, ~15 minutes start to plate. The egg-and-peas pairing carries real protein and fibre, and cumin, chilli and lemon keep an all-pantry dish genuinely savoury rather than bland; the temper and herb levers push the taste from good to great.
