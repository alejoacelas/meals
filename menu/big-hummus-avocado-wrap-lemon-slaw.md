## Big Hummus & Avocado Wrap with Lemon Slaw
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~10m · 1 session · ~10m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** knife (no stove needed)
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | wholemeal wrap, hummus, avocado, cabbage, carrot, pumpkin seeds, red wine vinegar, fresh lemon, chilli flakes, fine sea salt |
| **Time** | active ~10m · ~10m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | shred cabbage and carrot and toss with vinegar, salt and chilli → smash avocado with lemon and salt → spread hummus edge to edge on the wrap → pile on avocado, slaw and pumpkin seeds → roll tight and wrap in foil |

The one truly portable, eat-anywhere dish: no heat at all, just creamy hummus and lemony avocado against a crunchy, tangy slaw, rolled tight and wrapped in foil to go.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 large wholemeal wrap
- 3–4 tbsp hummus
- ½ ripe avocado
- A big handful cabbage, finely shredded
- ½ carrot, grated or cut into thin matchsticks
- 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- ½ lemon
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Fine sea salt

**Method**
1. Toss the shredded cabbage and carrot with the red wine vinegar, a good pinch of salt and the chilli flakes. Leave it to sit and soften while you do the rest.
2. Smash the avocado on a plate with a squeeze of lemon and a pinch of salt — keep it a bit chunky.
3. Spread the hummus edge to edge across the wrap, right to the rim, so every bite has some.
4. Smear the avocado down the middle, pile on the slaw (lift it out of its liquid so the wrap stays dry), and scatter over the pumpkin seeds.
5. Fold in the sides, roll up tight from the bottom, and cut in half — or wrap it in foil and take it with you.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Protein lift (+ feta, not in kit; or a sliced boiled egg):** crumble feta or fold in a sliced egg for a real protein bump and extra body.
- **Heat (+ pickled jalapeños or sriracha, not in kit):** a few slices or a squeeze of hot sauce for a sharp kick against the creamy fillings.
- **Warm & substantial (+ halloumi, not in kit):** grate and pan-sear a slab, then layer it in hot for a heartier, melty wrap.
- **Sturdier carry (+ corn tortilla or flatbread, not in kit):** swap to a thicker flatbread or double up if you're packing it for later.

**Why it scores well:** Dominant criterion is **low effort** — zero cooking, no stove, just a knife and a few minutes of assembly, then it travels in foil with no further attention (effort 🟢). Hummus, avocado and pumpkin seeds stack fibre and healthy fats with cabbage and carrot for vitamin C, K and beta-carotene on a wholemeal base; protein is moderate, which the egg or feta lever fixes (nutrition 🟢). Every item is a supermarket staple (availability 🟢), and the creamy-against-crunchy, lemony-tangy contrast makes it genuinely good as-is (taste 🟢).
