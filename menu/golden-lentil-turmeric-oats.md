## Golden Lentil & Turmeric Oats (Dal Porridge)
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟡 (active ~12m · 1 session · ~30m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟡
**Equipment:** one pot / knife
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | red split lentils, rolled oats, onion, garlic, ground turmeric, ground cumin, ground ginger, chilli flakes, spinach, rapeseed oil, fresh lemon, fine sea salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~12m · ~30m total · 1 session |

Savoury oats gone full khichdi: red split lentils simmered soft with turmeric, cumin and ginger, then oats stirred in to thicken everything into a golden porridge, spinach wilted through and lemon to finish. Lentil-plus-oat makes it a complete-protein, iron-rich one-pot bowl — the meat-free, egg-free flagship of the savoury breakfast side, and a clear step away from the cumin-peas-egg oats.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 80 g red split lentils, rinsed
- 40 g (~½ cup) rolled oats
- ½ onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- ¾ tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- A pinch of chilli flakes (plus more to finish)
- 2 big handfuls spinach
- ~600 ml water
- ½ lemon
- 1 tbsp rapeseed oil
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Rinse the lentils in a sieve until the water runs clear; set aside. Warm the oil in the pot over medium and soften the onion 3–4 min, then add the garlic, turmeric, cumin, ground ginger and chilli flakes and fry ~1 min until fragrant and deep gold.
2. Tip in the lentils and the water. Bring to a simmer and cook, stirring now and then so they don't catch, ~15 min, until the lentils swell and start to collapse into a loose dal.
3. Stir in the oats and simmer another 4–5 min, stirring, until thick and porridge-like. Loosen with a splash more water if it tightens past spoonable.
4. Fold the spinach through to wilt, ~1 min. Take off the heat, season hard with salt and black pepper, and squeeze in the lemon. Taste and push the cumin, chilli or lemon until it reads warm and bright. Serve hot with a final pinch of chilli.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **More protein on top (+ egg, in kit):** crown the bowl with a jammy or fried egg — the runny yolk enriches the porridge and pushes it past 25 g protein.
- **Real tadka (+ black mustard seeds, not in kit):** pop a teaspoon of mustard seeds in a little hot oil until they crackle, then pour the sizzling oil over each bowl — the defining finish of a proper dal.
- **Brighter, fresher dal (+ fresh ginger, green chilli & coriander, not in kit):** grate in fresh ginger and a sliced green chilli with the aromatics, and scatter torn coriander at the end for a livelier, more fragrant bowl.
- **Creamy finish (+ coconut milk, not in kit):** stir in a splash of coconut milk off the heat for a richer, rounder, gently sweet porridge.

**Why it scores well:** Nutrition leads — red lentils and oats together make a complete protein with serious fibre and iron, turmeric and ginger bring anti-inflammatory warmth, spinach adds folate and more iron, and rapeseed oil the healthy fat, all in one pot with nothing empty. It's ordinary shelf staples over one unbroken session (availability 🟢), though the ~15-minute lentil simmer plus the constant-stir oat phase keep effort at 🟡. Toasted spices, garlic and a hard squeeze of lemon make the water-based core genuinely good; an egg or a real tadka takes it to great (taste 🟡).
