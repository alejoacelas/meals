## Kale & Mushroom Sauté

**Equipment:** one pot / one pan / knife
**Serves:** 1–2

A work in progress. Two cheap, nutritious vegetables, a pot and a pan: blanch the
kale, steam the mushrooms off the same heat, then finish everything in a garlicky
sauté.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 bunch kale, stems stripped, torn
- A few handfuls mushrooms, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- Olive oil, salt
- Optional: chilli flakes, a squeeze of lemon

**Method**
1. Blanch the kale in boiling water ~1–2 min until just tender; lift it out.
2. Off the heat, add the mushrooms to the same hot pot to steam briefly.
3. In a pan, warm olive oil and soften the garlic; add mushrooms, then kale.
   Sauté until the mushrooms colour and any water cooks off. Salt; lemon if using.

### Level it up
- **Add a protein (in kit):** a fried egg on top, or warm chickpeas through.
- **Brown the mushrooms harder:** sear them dry and hard first for deeper flavour —
  costs a few minutes.
- **Skip the blanch:** sauté the kale directly to save a pot, if you don't mind it
  chewier.
- **Beyond the kit (not in kit):** fold a tin of white beans through to make it a
  fuller meal.

**Why it scores well:** Two cheap, nutritious vegetables; only a pot and a pan; one
session.

**Still iterating** (why taste is 🟡): is blanching worth the extra pot, or just
sauté the kale? Brown the mushrooms harder for flavour, or keep it quick?
