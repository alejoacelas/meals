## Spinach, Tomato & Chickpeas
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~10m · 1 session · ~15m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pan / knife
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | canned chickpeas, spinach, tomato, garlic, olive oil, salt |
| **Time** | active ~10m · ~15m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | soften garlic in oil → cook tomato down to a loose sauce → warm chickpeas through it → wilt spinach in, season (lemon/chilli optional) |

The staple. Fibre, plant protein and iron in one pan. Good even with nothing but
salt; better with garlic and olive oil.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 2 handfuls spinach (fresh or frozen)
- 1 tomato, chopped (or a few tinned / cherry tomatoes)
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- Olive oil, salt
- Optional: chilli flakes, cumin, a squeeze of lemon

**Method**
1. Warm a good glug of olive oil in the pan; soften the garlic ~1 min.
2. Add tomato; cook down 2–3 min to a loose sauce.
3. Add chickpeas; warm through and let them take on the sauce, 3–4 min.
4. Stir in spinach until just wilted. Salt, plus lemon / chilli if using.

### Level it up
- **Make it a full meal with pasta (in kit):** boil fusilli or penne (not
  spaghetti — short shapes fit the pot and don't make a mess), drain, and fold
  through. Adds ~2m active, ~+10m total for the boil.
- **Swap the greens or beans (in kit):** kale for spinach, or stir red split
  lentils in with the tomato for a thicker, higher-protein pan.
- **Beyond the kit (not in kit):** white beans for the chickpeas, or chard for the
  spinach; crumble feta over at the end for salty richness.

**Why it scores well:** Pantry-stable, one pan, one continuous session, all
supermarket ingredients, genuinely tasty even unspiced.
