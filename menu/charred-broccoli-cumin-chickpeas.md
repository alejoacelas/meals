## Charred Broccoli & Crispy Cumin Chickpeas
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟡 (active ~12m · 1 session · ~20m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pan / knife
**Serves:** 1–2

| | |
|---|---|
| **Core ingredients** | broccoli, canned chickpeas, garlic, ground cumin, chilli flakes, extra-virgin olive oil, lemon, salt, black pepper |
| **Time** | active ~12m · ~20m total · 1 session |
| **Steps** | dry-fry chickpeas nutty and crisp → char broccoli hard in oil → toss in garlic, cumin, chilli → return chickpeas, hard squeeze of lemon, salt, pepper |

The vegan brassica powerhouse of the set: chickpeas dry-fried until crisp, broccoli charred to deep brown, pulled together with garlic, cumin and a hard hit of lemon. Eats just as well cold the next day.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 can chickpeas, drained and patted dry
- 1 head broccoli, cut into small florets (slice any thick stem thin)
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ lemon
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Heat the pan dry over medium-high; add the chickpeas and dry-fry, shaking now and then, until nutty and crisp, ~8 min. Salt, then tip onto a plate.
2. Add a good glug of olive oil and the broccoli, cut-side down. Leave it largely undisturbed to char hard, ~5–6 min; a splash of water and a brief cover helps the stems soften, then let it cook dry and colour.
3. Drop the heat, push the broccoli aside, and add the garlic, cumin and chilli flakes; toss ~1 min until fragrant.
4. Return the chickpeas, toss to combine, and take off the heat. Squeeze over the lemon, season with salt and plenty of black pepper.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Tahini-lemon drizzle (+ tahini, not in kit):** loosen tahini with lemon juice and a little water to a pourable sauce; spoon over for a creamy, nutty finish.
- **Salty-cool contrast (+ feta, not in kit):** crumble feta over the warm pan so it half-melts against the charred edges.
- **More crunch (+ flaked almonds or pumpkin seeds, not in kit):** toast in the dry pan before the chickpeas and scatter on at the end for extra healthy fat.
- **Sticky-spicy glaze (+ honey and soy sauce, not in kit):** off the heat, add a drizzle of honey and a splash of soy sauce; toss so it clings to the chickpeas as a chilli-sweet glaze.

**Why it scores well:** Nutrition leads — broccoli brings vitamin C, K, folate and fibre, chickpeas add plant protein and iron, olive oil the healthy fat. One pan, one session; every core item is an ordinary supermarket staple; and char plus cumin plus lemon makes it genuinely tasty hot or cold.
