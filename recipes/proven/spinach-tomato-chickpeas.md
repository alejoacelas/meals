## Spinach, Tomato & Chickpeas
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟢 (active ~10m · 1 session · ~15m total) ·
**Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pan, knife
**Serves:** 1–2

The staple. Fibre, plant protein, and iron in one pan. Good even with nothing but
salt; better with garlic and olive oil.

### Ingredients
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 2 handfuls spinach (fresh or frozen)
- 1 tomato, chopped (or a few tinned / cherry tomatoes)
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- Olive oil, salt
- Optional: chilli flakes, cumin, a squeeze of lemon

### Method
1. Warm a good glug of olive oil in the pan; soften the garlic ~1 min.
2. Add tomato; cook down 2–3 min to a loose sauce.
3. Add chickpeas; warm through and let them take on the sauce, 3–4 min.
4. Stir in spinach until just wilted. Salt, plus lemon / chilli if using.

**Why it scores well:** Pantry-stable, one pan, one continuous session, all
supermarket ingredients, genuinely tasty even unspiced.
**Levers:**
- **Make it a full meal with pasta:** boil fusilli or penne (not spaghetti — it
  fits the pot and doesn't make a mess), drain, and fold through. Adds ~2m active,
  ~+10m total for the boil.
- Swap chickpeas for white beans; spinach for chard.
