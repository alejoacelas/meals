# Candidates — the recipe catalog

24 untested recipes from the recipe workflow (`../../workflow.md`), each scored
against the four principles (`../../CLAUDE.md`). They share one rule: the **core
recipe uses only proven ingredients** — the minimal version cooks from the kit in
`../../ingredients/proven/` alone. Every *Level it up* lever is a widely-available
extra, flagged `(not in kit)`, so you can make any dish plainer or fancier on the day.

All drafts — nothing's been cooked yet. Promotion to `../proven/` is by hand: cook one,
and if it earns its place, move the file up. Never automatic.

Scores: 🟢 great · 🟡 ok/mixed · 🔴 weak, across **Nutrition · Effort · Availability · Taste**.
(Availability is 🟢 throughout — every core is all-proven.)

## By type
### Quick one-pan dinners
- ⭐ **[Chickpea Shakshuka](chickpea-shakshuka.md)** — Eggs poached into a cumin-spiced tomato-and-chickpea sauce — a complete, restaurant-feeling, protein-rich dinner from a single pan.
- ⭐ **[Garlicky Mushrooms & Spinach with Runny Eggs](garlicky-mushrooms-spinach-runny-eggs.md)** — The kale-mushroom sauté grown up: hard-seared umami mushrooms and wilted spinach under runny yolks, now a complete egg-protein meal in one pan.
- **[Charred Broccoli & Crispy Cumin Chickpeas](charred-broccoli-cumin-chickpeas.md)** — The vegan brassica powerhouse of the set: chickpeas dry-fried until crisp, broccoli charred to deep brown, pulled together with garlic, cumin and a hard hit of lemon.

### Pasta
- ⭐ **[Aglio e Olio with Greens & Chickpeas](aglio-e-olio-greens-chickpeas.md)** — The Roman classic, made a full meal: garlic, oil, chilli and lemon whipped with starchy pasta water into a glossy sauce, with chickpeas and broccoli carrying the protein, fibre and bite.
- **[Frizzled Cabbage & Chickpea Pasta](frizzled-cabbage-chickpea-pasta.md)** — Cabbage fried hard until the edges go sweet, charred and nutty, with cumin-warmed chickpeas folded through pasta.
- **[Pasta e Ceci (One-Pot Chickpea Pasta)](pasta-e-ceci.md)** — The pasta cooks right in a garlicky chickpea-tomato broth, with a third of the chickpeas mashed so the whole thing turns silky and creamy.

### Eggs & breakfast
- ⭐ **[Frozen Pea & Egg, Lemon-Bright](frozen-pea-egg-lemon-bright.md)** — Sweet peas straight from the freezer, soft eggs and a hit of lemon — bright, satisfying, and not a single thing to chop.
- **[Savoury Cumin Oats with Peas & Egg](savoury-cumin-oats-peas-egg.md)** — An upma-inspired savoury porridge: oats tempered with cumin, onion and garlic, sweetened by peas, finished with a soft egg.
- **[Tomato & Egg (番茄炒蛋)](tomato-and-egg.md)** — The beloved Chinese home classic: silky eggs folded through a sweet-tangy tomato sauce.

### No-cook & assembly
- **[Chickpea & Egg Deli Salad](chickpea-egg-deli-salad.md)** — A room-temperature deli salad with double protein: creamy egg and chickpeas brightened by tomato and vinegar-quick-pickled onion.
- **[Lemony Smashed-Chickpea & Jammy-Egg Bowl](lemony-smashed-chickpea-jammy-egg-bowl.md)** — The lowest-effort way to get a hot protein on the plate: you boil an egg, and the rest is fork-and-assemble.

### Soups & stews
- **[Carrot, Cumin & Chickpea Pottage](carrot-cumin-chickpea-pottage.md)** — A tomato-free, naturally sweet bowl: carrots and chickpeas simmered soft, then hand-mashed to a velvety pottage — no blender, no fuss.
- **[Peppery Mushroom & Chickpea Stew](peppery-mushroom-chickpea-stew.md)** — Hard-browned mushrooms build a meaty, umami broth; mashing some of the chickpeas thickens it into a ragout you can eat as a bowl or spoon straight over pasta.
- **[Smoky Cumin Chickpea Chilli](smoky-cumin-chickpea-chilli.md)** — A thick one-pot chilli where bloomed cumin and a hard-browned onion stand in for a whole spice rack — and it only gets better reheated the next day.
- **[Tomato Egg-Drop Soup](tomato-egg-drop-soup.md)** — Silky egg ribbons set into a tangy tomato broth in fifteen minutes — the fastest hot bowl in the kit, and the only soup that leans on eggs for its protein.

### Batch & make-ahead
- **[Chickpea & Vegetable Minestrone Soup](chickpea-vegetable-minestrone-soup.md)** — A chop-and-simmer pot of five veg and a legume that freezes and reheats better than almost anything.
- **[Stovetop Frittata Cake (onion, pepper & spinach)](stovetop-frittata-cake.md)** — A thick egg cake set slowly under a lid and flipped on a plate — no oven needed — then cut into wedges you eat warm now and cold from the fridge all week.
- **[Sweet-&-Sour Braised Cabbage with Chickpeas](sweet-sour-braised-cabbage-chickpeas.md)** — Cabbage braised slow until it collapses sweet and glossy, then lifted bright with red-wine vinegar.

### Stir-fries & Asian-leaning
- ⭐ **[Black-Pepper Mushroom & Pak Choi with Egg](black-pepper-mushroom-pak-choi-egg.md)** — Hard-browned mushrooms and wilted pak choi under a fried egg, heavy on cracked black pepper — deep, meaty umami without a drop of soy, the runny yolk doing the work of a sauce.
- **[Charred Cabbage & Egg Pan](charred-cabbage-egg-pan.md)** — Hard-charred cabbage turns sweet and nutty against soft scrambled egg, with garlic and chilli running through it.
- **[Cumin Pepper, Carrot & Chickpea Stir-fry](cumin-pepper-carrot-chickpea-stir-fry.md)** — A Xinjiang-leaning stir-fry: peppers and carrots blistered hard, crisp-edged chickpeas, and a heavy hand of cumin and chilli.

### Salads & bowls
- ⭐ **[Jammy Eggs over Tomato & Spinach Salad](jammy-eggs-tomato-spinach-salad.md)** — A raw tomato-and-spinach salad with halved jammy eggs on top: the only cooking is the pot of boiling water, and the soft runny yolk breaks down into the dressing as you eat.
- **[Crunchy Cabbage & Carrot Slaw with Cumin Chickpeas](crunchy-cabbage-carrot-slaw-cumin-chickpeas.md)** — A no-cook slaw you shred once and live off for days: cabbage and carrot stay crunchy, cumin and chilli warm the dressing, and chickpeas turn it into a real meal.
- **[Lemon-Dressed Chickpea & Raw Veg Bowl](lemon-dressed-chickpea-raw-veg-bowl.md)** — The flagship no-cook bowl: a tin of chickpeas, a handful of raw veg, and a sharp lemon-cumin dressing tossed in one bowl.
