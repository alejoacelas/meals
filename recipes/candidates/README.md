# Candidates — the recipe catalog

42 untested recipes from the recipe workflow (`../../workflow.md`), each scored
against the four principles (`../../CLAUDE.md`). They share one rule: the **core
recipe uses only proven ingredients** — the minimal version cooks from the kit in
`../../ingredients/proven/` alone. Every *Level it up* lever is a widely-available
extra, flagged `(not in kit)`, so you can make any dish plainer or fancier on the day.

All drafts — nothing's been cooked yet. Promotion to `../proven/` is by hand: cook one,
and if it earns its place, move the file up. Never automatic.

Scores: 🟢 great · 🟡 ok/mixed · 🔴 weak, across **Nutrition · Effort · Availability · Taste**.
(Availability is 🟢 throughout — every core is all-proven.)

## By type
### Quick one-pan dinners
- ⭐ **[Garlicky Mushrooms & Spinach with Runny Eggs](garlicky-mushrooms-spinach-runny-eggs.md)** — The kale-mushroom sauté grown up: hard-seared umami mushrooms and wilted spinach under runny yolks, now a complete egg-protein meal in one pan.

### Pasta & noodles
- **[Aglio e Olio with Greens & Chickpeas](aglio-e-olio-greens-chickpeas.md)** — The Roman classic, made a full meal: garlic, oil, chilli and lemon whipped with starchy pasta water into a glossy sauce, with chickpeas and broccoli carrying the protein, fibre and bite.
- **[Frizzled Cabbage & Chickpea Pasta](frizzled-cabbage-chickpea-pasta.md)** — Cabbage fried hard until the edges go sweet, charred and nutty, with cumin-warmed chickpeas folded through pasta.
- **[Pasta e Ceci (One-Pot Chickpea Pasta)](pasta-e-ceci.md)** — The pasta cooks right in a garlicky chickpea-tomato broth, with a third of the chickpeas mashed so the whole thing turns silky and creamy.
