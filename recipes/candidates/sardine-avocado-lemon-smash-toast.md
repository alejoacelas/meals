## Sardine, Avocado & Lemon Smash on Seeded Toast

The fastest omega-3 hit in the set and the one you'd actually crave: ripe avocado smashed onto garlic-rubbed seeded toast, tinned sardines forked in with a big squeeze of lemon, chilli and oil over the top. The creamy avocado fat rounds off the strong fish and carries the lemon; the seeded bread gives bite and fibre. The only heat is the toaster.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 2 slices seeded wholemeal bread
- 1 tin sardines in oil (~120g), drained
- 1 ripe avocado
- 1 clove garlic, halved
- ½ lemon
- ¼ onion, very thinly sliced
- 1 tsp red wine vinegar
- Chilli flakes, to taste
- 1 tbsp pumpkin seeds
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Toss the sliced onion with the red wine vinegar and a pinch of salt in a small bowl; leave it to soften and turn sharp-sweet while you do everything else.
2. Toast the bread until deep golden — in a dry pan over medium heat (a couple of minutes a side) or in a toaster. Rub each hot slice with the cut face of the garlic and drizzle with a little olive oil.
3. Halve the avocado, scoop onto the toast, and mash it down with a fork. Squeeze over a little lemon and season with salt and pepper.
4. Fork the sardines over the avocado in big flakes, breaking them just enough to spread, and squeeze on more lemon.
5. Scatter over the pickled onion, chilli flakes and pumpkin seeds, finish with a thread of olive oil and a grind of pepper, and eat straight away while the toast is crisp.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Heat underneath (+ harissa or sriracha, not in kit):** a thin smear under the avocado adds a warm, smoky background that loves the oily fish.
- **Briny pop (+ capers or chopped gherkins, not in kit):** a scatter of either cuts the richness and makes it taste more like a proper sardine tartine.
- **Herb lift (+ fresh dill or parsley, not in kit):** a pinch torn over the top brightens everything and pairs naturally with sardines.
- **Better bread (+ sourdough or rye, not in kit):** a sturdy slice toasts crisper and holds the smash without going soggy.

**Why it scores well:** Effort leads, and hard — one board, a fork and the toaster, done in under ten minutes with nothing to watch or wash up. Nutrition rides along for free: bone-in sardines bring omega-3, B12, vitamin D and calcium, the avocado adds healthy fat and potassium, and the seeded bread plus pumpkin seeds pile on fibre. Every item is an ordinary supermarket staple, and the lemon-against-creamy-avocado-against-salty-fish balance makes it genuinely crave-able.
