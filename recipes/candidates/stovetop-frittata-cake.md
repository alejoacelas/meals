## Stovetop Frittata Cake (onion, pepper & spinach)

**Equipment:** one pan + lid, a plate (for the flip), knife
**Serves:** 1–2 per sitting (makes 4–6 wedges)

A thick egg cake set slowly under a lid and flipped on a plate — no oven needed — then cut into wedges you eat warm now and cold from the fridge all week. One session buys you the cleanest make-ahead protein in the catalog.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 6 eggs
- 1 onion, finely sliced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 large handfuls spinach (fresh, roughly chopped; or frozen, thawed and squeezed dry)
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Warm a good glug of oil in the pan over medium heat. Soften the onion and pepper, stirring now and then, until sweet and floppy, ~8 min.
2. Add the garlic for ~1 min, then the spinach; let it wilt and any water cook off, ~1–2 min. You want the veg fairly dry. Spread it in an even layer.
3. Beat the eggs with a good pinch of salt, plenty of black pepper and the chilli flakes. Pour over the veg, give one gentle stir to settle, then drop the heat to low and cover.
4. Cook gently, lid on, until the cake is set around the edge and only just soft on top, ~15–18 min. Don't rush the heat or the base will scorch before the middle sets.
5. Loosen the edges, slide the cake onto a plate, invert the pan over it and flip it back in. Cook the second side 2–3 min to set.
6. Slide onto a plate to cool. Cut into wedges; eat warm, or keep in the fridge up to 4–5 days and eat cold.

*Stretch it within the kit:* stir a handful of frozen peas into the veg, or soften sliced mushroom alongside the onion — both are core-palette and add bulk and fibre.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Salty anchor (+ feta, cheddar or parmesan, not in kit):** crumble or grate through the egg before it sets — the savoury hit that lifts taste from good to genuinely moreish, cold or warm.
- **Spanish tortilla (+ boiled potato, not in kit):** layer in thin slices of pre-boiled potato with the veg for a more filling, classic tortilla wedge.
- **Smoky meat (+ chorizo, not in kit):** fry a little diced chorizo first and build the frittata in its red, spiced oil for deep flavour throughout.
- **Fresh + smoky finish (+ smoked paprika and/or fresh parsley, not in kit):** a teaspoon of paprika in the eggs adds warmth and colour; torn parsley over the wedges keeps it bright.

**Why it scores well:** Effort and nutrition lead together. One unbroken session — chop, soften, pour, cover, flip — yields four to six wedges, so the per-meal effort is tiny and there's nothing to reheat or assemble later. Each wedge stacks complete protein, choline and B12 from the eggs with healthy fat and a real serving of veg (spinach iron and folate, pepper vitamin C, sweet onion). Every core item is an ordinary supermarket and freezer staple, cooked with just a pan and a plate. Taste is solid rather than spectacular eaten plain and cold — which is exactly what the cheese, chorizo and paprika levers are there to fix.
