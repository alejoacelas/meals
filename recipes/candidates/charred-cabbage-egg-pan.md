## Charred Cabbage & Egg Pan

Hard-charred cabbage turns sweet and nutty against soft scrambled egg, with garlic and chilli running through it. The cheapest veg on the shelf, on the plate in about a quarter of an hour — it earns its place on taste and how little it asks of you.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- ¼ cabbage, cut into rough 2 cm ribbons (a few big handfuls)
- 3 eggs
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- A splash of red wine vinegar
- Rapeseed oil, fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and a grind of pepper.
2. Heat a glug of oil in the pan over high heat. Pour in the eggs and scramble in big soft folds until just set, ~1 min. Lift them out while still glossy.
3. Add a little more oil, then the cabbage in as flat a layer as you can. Leave it undisturbed to catch and char, then toss and leave again, repeating until the edges blacken and the cabbage tastes sweet — 8–10 min.
4. Add the garlic and chilli and toss for ~30s until fragrant.
5. Splash in the red wine vinegar to deglaze and lift the char off the pan, then fold the eggs back through to warm. Season with salt and plenty of black pepper.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Egg-fried-rice depth (+ soy sauce, + sesame oil, both not in kit):** a splash of soy with the vinegar and a few drops of sesame oil off the heat give it savoury, toasty backbone.
- **Aromatic lift (+ spring onion, + ginger, both not in kit):** grate in a little ginger with the garlic and scatter sliced spring onion over at the end.
- **Make it a meal (+ cooked rice, not in kit):** fold a couple of handfuls of cold cooked rice in after the cabbage chars to turn it into a proper egg fried rice.
- **More protein (+ edamame or firm tofu, not in kit):** toss frozen edamame or cubed tofu in with the cabbage for extra protein and bite.

**Why it scores well:** Dominant criterion is **taste + effort** — hard charring is the one trick that makes cheap cabbage genuinely crave-able, and it all happens in one pan in a single unbroken session (effort 🟢). Eggs bring complete protein, choline and B12 alongside cabbage's fibre and vitamin C/K; nutrition is 🟡 only for being light on protein and grain-free, which the edamame/tofu and rice levers fix. Cabbage, eggs and garlic are everywhere (availability 🟢).
