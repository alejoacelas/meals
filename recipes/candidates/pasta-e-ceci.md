## Pasta e Ceci (One-Pot Chickpea Pasta)
**Nutrition** 🟢 · **Effort** 🟡 (active ~16m · 1 session · ~26m total) · **Availability** 🟢 · **Taste** 🟢
**Equipment:** one pot, knife
**Serves:** 1–2

The pasta cooks right in a garlicky chickpea-tomato broth, with a third of the chickpeas mashed so the whole thing turns silky and creamy. One pot, nothing to drain, deeply savoury — pantry food that eats like a treat.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 150g pasta (fusilli or penne — short shapes fit the pot)
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- ½ tin chopped tomatoes (about 200g)
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, sliced
- Olive oil
- Chilli flakes
- ~500ml water, plus more if needed
- Salt, black pepper
- Optional (still core): 2 handfuls spinach, stirred in at the end for a green

**Method**
1. Warm a good glug of olive oil in the pot; soften the onion 4–5 min, then add the garlic and a pinch of chilli flakes for 1 min.
2. Stir in the chopped tomatoes; cook down 3–4 min to a thick, jammy paste.
3. Add the chickpeas and mash about a third of them against the side of the pot — this is what makes the broth creamy.
4. Pour in the water, season with salt, and bring to a steady simmer.
5. Stir in the pasta. Simmer 10–12 min, stirring often so it doesn't catch, until the pasta is tender and the broth has thickened around it. Loosen with a splash of water if it tightens too far. Stir through the spinach to wilt, if using. Finish with black pepper and a drizzle of olive oil.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Cheese it (+ parmesan, not in kit):** grate parmesan over each bowl, and drop a rind into the broth at step 4 to simmer — deep, savoury umami for almost no effort.
- **Herb backbone (+ fresh rosemary or a bay leaf, not in kit):** add a small sprig of rosemary or a bay leaf with the tomatoes; fish it out before serving for a warm, aromatic lift.
- **More legume body (+ tinned cannellini or tinned lentils, not in kit):** swap or top up some of the chickpeas — cannellini melt creamier, lentils add an earthy bite.
- **Silk finish (+ a splash of cream or yoghurt, not in kit):** stir a spoonful through off the heat for a rounder, richer broth.

**Why it scores well:** Everything is ordinary-supermarket, all-proven stock (availability 🟢), and the mash-a-third trick turns a flat pantry into a genuinely creamy, savoury bowl (taste 🟢). Chickpeas carry plant protein and fibre, with tomato and olive oil rounding out the nutrition — better still with the optional spinach (🟢). It's one pot and one session with nothing to drain, but the pasta cooks in the broth and wants frequent stirring so it doesn't catch, so effort is good rather than effortless (🟡).
