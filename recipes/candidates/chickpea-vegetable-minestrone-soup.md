## Chickpea & Vegetable Minestrone Soup

A chop-and-simmer pot of five veg and a legume that freezes and reheats better
than almost anything. Cook the pasta separately and drop it in per bowl, so the
soup keeps for a week without the noodles bloating.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- ¼ cabbage, shredded (a few handfuls)
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- 1 can chopped tomatoes (~400 g)
- 1 can chickpeas, drained
- 2 handfuls frozen peas
- 2 handfuls spinach (fresh or frozen)
- ~1 L water
- 150 g pasta (fusilli or penne), cooked separately
- Extra-virgin olive oil, chilli flakes, fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Warm a good glug of olive oil in the pot; sweat the onion and carrot over
   medium heat, 6–8 min, until softening and sweet.
2. Stir in the garlic and a pinch of chilli flakes; cook 1 min until fragrant.
3. Pour in the tomatoes and water, season with salt and pepper, and bring to a
   simmer.
4. Add the cabbage and chickpeas; simmer gently, stirring now and then, ~20 min,
   until everything is tender and the broth tastes round.
5. Stir in the peas and spinach and cook 2 min more, just to wilt. Taste and
   adjust salt, pepper and chilli.
6. Cook the pasta separately in salted water, drain, and add a scoop to each bowl
   as you serve (keep the rest of the pasta apart so the soup stores well).

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Savoury backbone (+ stock cube, not in kit):** crumble one into the water in
  step 3 — the single biggest taste upgrade, turning plain broth into proper soup.
- **Umami depth (+ parmesan rind + grated parmesan, not in kit):** simmer a rind
  in the broth for salty richness, then grate fresh parmesan over each bowl.
- **More legume (+ tinned cannellini or borlotti beans, not in kit):** add a
  drained tin alongside or in place of the chickpeas for a softer, creamier body.
- **Bright herby finish (+ pesto, not in kit):** swirl a spoonful into each bowl
  at the table; or bulk it out with (+ diced potato or kale, not in kit) added
  with the cabbage.

**Why it scores well:** Dominant criterion is **nutrition** — five vegetables plus
chickpeas stack protein, fibre, iron and vitamin A/C/K into every bowl. It's all
ordinary shelf staples in one pot (availability 🟢), and though a single cook runs
a little long, it's one unbroken session that pays back across a week of reheats
(effort 🟡). Honest, hearty and good on water alone; a stock cube or parmesan rind
lifts the taste from solid to great.
