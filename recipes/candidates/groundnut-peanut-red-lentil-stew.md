## Groundnut Peanut & Red Lentil Stew

A West-African-style one-pot: red lentils simmered into tomato and a warm curry-paprika-ginger base, then enriched with a heaped spoon of peanut butter that melts the whole thing creamy, with greens wilted through. Pantry spices alone carry it — the peanut-tomato richness does the heavy lifting.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 150g red split lentils, rinsed
- 1 heaped tbsp peanut butter
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- ½ tin chopped tomatoes (about 200g)
- 1½ tsp curry powder
- 1 tsp smoked or sweet paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- Chilli flakes, to taste
- 2 big handfuls spinach and/or kale (kale stems removed), roughly chopped
- Rapeseed oil
- ~600ml water, plus more if needed
- Fine sea salt

**Method**
1. Warm a glug of rapeseed oil in the pot over medium heat. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook 5–6 min until soft and golden at the edges.
2. Drop the heat, add the garlic, curry powder, paprika, cumin, ground ginger and chilli flakes, and stir ~1 min until the spices smell toasty — don't let them scorch.
3. Stir in the chopped tomatoes and cook 2–3 min to a thick paste, then tip in the lentils and the water. Season with salt and bring to a simmer.
4. Simmer 20–25 min, stirring now and then so the lentils don't catch on the base, until they've collapsed into a soft, thick stew. Add a splash of water if it tightens too far.
5. Stir in the peanut butter until it melts through and the stew turns creamy and glossy. Fold in the greens and cook 2–3 min until wilted (kale a touch longer than spinach). Taste for salt, chilli and a little more peanut butter if you want it richer.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Savoury depth (+ stock cube or bouillon and tomato purée, not in kit):** crumble a stock cube into the water and fry a spoon of tomato purée with the spices for a rounder, more savoury base.
- **Rounder and richer (+ coconut milk, not in kit):** stir a splash through at the end for a silkier, mellower stew that tames the chilli.
- **Fresh lift (+ fresh ginger, chilli, lime and coriander, not in kit):** grate in fresh ginger and chilli with the garlic, then finish with a squeeze of lime and torn coriander to cut the richness.
- **Make it a plate (+ rice and roasted peanuts, not in kit):** spoon over rice and scatter crushed roasted peanuts on top for crunch.

**Why it scores well:** Nutrition leads — red lentils bring plant protein and fibre, peanut butter adds healthy fat and more protein, and the spinach and kale load it with micronutrients, all from tomato-rich one-pot comfort. Every item is an ordinary shelf staple and all-proven (availability 🟢), and the peanut-tomato base tastes far slower-cooked than it is (taste 🟢). It's one pot in one session, but lentils want ~25 min of occasional stirring so they don't catch, so effort is good rather than effortless (🟡).
