A quick satay-style bowl: crisp pan-fried tofu and a fast cabbage-carrot-pak choi stir-fry turned glossy in a creamy, savoury, lemon-sharp peanut sauce. The sauce is the whole draw — and it proves peanut butter pulls its weight as a serious savoury main, not just a toast spread.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 block firm tofu (~280g), patted very dry, cut into 2cm cubes
- 2 heaped tbsp peanut butter
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed or finely grated
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- A good pinch of chilli flakes
- Juice of ½ lemon (plus a wedge to finish)
- Rapeseed oil
- ¼ small cabbage, shredded
- 2 heads pak choi, stems and leaves separated, stems sliced
- 1 carrot, cut into thin matchsticks
- A handful of pumpkin seeds
- Fine sea salt
- Water

**Method**
1. Make the sauce: in a mug or small bowl, whisk the peanut butter, garlic, ground ginger, chilli flakes, lemon juice and a pinch of salt with warm water, a splash at a time, until smooth and pourable like single cream. Set aside.
2. Heat a good glug of rapeseed oil in the pan over medium-high. Add the tofu and fry, turning every couple of minutes, until deeply golden and crisp on several sides, ~8–10 min. Salt, then tip onto a plate.
3. Add a little more oil and turn the heat high. Add the carrot and cabbage and stir-fry hard, ~3–4 min, with a splash of water to steam them tender-crisp. Add the pak choi stems for a minute, then the leaves, and toss until just wilted.
4. Return the tofu, pour over the peanut sauce and toss to glaze everything (loosen with a splash of water if it tightens). Scatter pumpkin seeds over the top and serve with the lemon wedge.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Proper satay balance (+ soy sauce, + lime, + honey, not in kit):** a splash of soy for salt, lime in place of lemon, and a teaspoon of honey whisked into the sauce gives the full salty-sour-sweet trio that makes satay sing.
- **Heat & aroma (+ sriracha, + sesame oil, not in kit):** a squeeze of sriracha into the sauce and a few drops of sesame oil swirled in off the heat for a nutty, fiery lift.
- **Make it a noodle bowl (+ rice noodles, not in kit; or pasta, in kit):** toss cooked rice noodles or fusilli through at the end so the peanut sauce coats them — a fuller, more filling plate.
- **Fresh top (+ coriander, spring onion, crushed peanuts, not in kit):** torn coriander, sliced spring onion and a few crushed peanuts scattered over for freshness and extra crunch.
