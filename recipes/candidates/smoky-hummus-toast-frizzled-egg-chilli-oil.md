## Smoky Hummus Toast with Frizzled Egg & Chilli Oil

A café plate in five minutes: thick hummus on hot toast, a crispy-edged frizzled egg on top, and a quick paprika-chilli oil poured over so it pools into the hummus. Hummus does the work of cooked chickpeas with none of the cooking, so it's the fastest hot, complete-protein dish in the set.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 2 slices wholemeal / seeded bread
- 4 tbsp hummus (a thick layer)
- 2 eggs
- Extra-virgin olive oil (a generous 2–3 tbsp — the egg frizzles in it)
- ½ tsp smoked paprika
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- A squeeze of fresh lemon
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Toast the bread — in a toaster, or dry in the pan over medium heat until crisp on both sides. Spread each slice thickly with hummus and set on your plate.
2. Wipe the pan, add the olive oil and set over medium-high until it shimmers. Crack in the eggs and let them sit undisturbed so the edges blister, bubble and frizzle into crisp lace; tilt the pan and spoon hot oil over the whites until set but the yolks stay runny, 1–2 min. Lay an egg on each hummus toast.
3. Drop the heat to low. Into the oil left in the pan (top up with a splash if dry), stir the smoked paprika and chilli flakes and bloom for 15–20 sec — just until fragrant and the oil turns red; don't let the paprika scorch.
4. Spoon the warm chilli oil over the eggs and toast so it pools into the hummus. Squeeze over lemon, season with salt and black pepper, and eat straight away.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Nutty crunch (+ dukkah or za'atar, not in kit):** a spoon scattered over at the end adds toasty, seedy texture against the soft hummus and yolk.
- **A hidden kick (+ harissa or sriracha, not in kit):** smear a thin line under the hummus for a deeper, fermented heat beneath the bloomed oil.
- **Bulk it out (+ avocado, in kit, or sliced fresh tomato, not in kit):** fan over the hummus before the egg for more healthy fat or a fresh, juicy layer.
- **Better bread (+ sourdough or an English muffin, not in kit):** swap in for a chewier, more structural base that holds the oil without going soggy.
