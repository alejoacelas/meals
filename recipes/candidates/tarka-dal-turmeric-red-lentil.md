The benchmark red-lentil dish and the one to learn first: lentils simmered soft with turmeric until they collapse, then finished with a tarka — garlic, cumin and chilli sizzled in hot oil and poured over so it crackles. That 60-second sizzle is the whole trick that makes cheap lentils crave-worthy, and it's the single most useful spice technique in the kit.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 150g red split lentils
- 1 onion, finely diced
- 3 cloves garlic, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1½ tsp ground cumin (split: 1 tsp for the pot, ½ tsp for the tarka)
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp ground ginger
- chilli flakes, to taste
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ lemon
- 1 bay leaf
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Rinse the lentils in a sieve or by swirling in the pot and pouring off the cloudy water two or three times.
2. Warm a glug of olive oil in the pot over medium heat and soften the onion with a pinch of salt, ~5 min, until translucent — you want it soft and sweet, not browned.
3. Add the lentils, 600ml water, turmeric, 1 tsp cumin, the coriander, ginger and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then drop to a low simmer for 20–25 min, stirring now and then and skimming off any foam, until the lentils slump into a loose porridge. Splash in more water if it tightens too much.
4. Season well with salt and black pepper, then whisk briskly to break the lentils smooth and loosen to a pourable-but-spoonable consistency. Squeeze in lemon to taste. Pull out the bay leaf and keep the dal warm on the lowest heat.
5. **The tarka:** in the small pan, heat 2 tbsp olive oil over medium-high until it shimmers. Add the sliced garlic and fry 30–45s until pale gold, then take the pan off the heat and tip in the remaining ½ tsp cumin and the chilli flakes — they'll crackle and bloom in the hot oil. Pour the whole sizzling lot, oil and all, over the dal. Stir half through and leave the rest pooled on top. Serve as is, or with bread or rice.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Richer tarka (+ butter or ghee, not in kit):** melt a knob into the hot oil before the garlic for a rounder, restaurant-style finish.
- **Brighter, sharper dal (+ fresh ginger and green chilli, not in kit):** grate fresh ginger into the simmer and slice a green chilli into the tarka for a livelier heat than the ground spices give.
- **Classic South Indian finish (+ black mustard seeds and curry leaves, not in kit):** start the tarka with mustard seeds until they pop, then add a few curry leaves with the garlic.
- **Veg-loaded dal (spinach or tomato, in kit):** wilt a couple of handfuls of spinach into the finished dal, or stir a chopped fresh tomato in with the lentils for a fuller, brothier bowl.

**Why it scores well:** Nutrition leads — red lentils pack protein and fibre per serving for almost nothing, with turmeric and olive oil along for the ride, and it's naturally vegan and reheats brilliantly. Taste is what makes it a keeper: the poured-over tarka turns a plain pot of lentils crave-worthy. Every item is an ordinary shelf staple, and it's essentially one pot in one continuous session.
