A raw tomato-and-spinach salad with halved jammy eggs on top: the only cooking is the pot of boiling water, and the soft runny yolk breaks down into the dressing as you eat. Built for taste on a genuinely nutritious base.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 2–3 eggs
- 1 large tomato, chopped (or a handful of cherry / tinned tomatoes)
- 2 big handfuls spinach (fresh; tear if leaves are large)
- ¼ onion, very thinly sliced
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- ½ lemon
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Extra-virgin olive oil, fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Lower the eggs into a pot of gently boiling water and simmer 6½ min for jammy yolks (set white, soft runny centre). Lift out and sit in cold water until cool enough to peel.
2. While they cook, toss the sliced onion with the vinegar and a pinch of salt in your serving bowl — leave it to soften and lose its raw bite.
3. Add the tomato and spinach. Dress with a good glug of olive oil, a squeeze of lemon, the chilli flakes, salt and black pepper, and toss to coat and slightly wilt the spinach.
4. Peel the eggs and halve them over the salad, cut-side up. Sprinkle the yolks with a little salt, pepper and chilli, then break into them as you eat so the yolk runs through as a rich dressing.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Creamy body (+ avocado, not in kit):** cube and fold through for buttery richness and healthy fat that plays off the yolk.
- **Salty brine (+ feta or olives, not in kit):** crumble feta or scatter olives for a savoury, briny edge and extra protein.
- **Omega-3 protein (+ tinned sardines or anchovy, not in kit):** drape a few sardine fillets over, or melt a chopped anchovy into the dressing, to make it a fuller meal.
- **Make it a plate (+ toast or croutons, not in kit):** serve over toast or scatter torn croutons in for crunch and to mop the yolk.

**Why it scores well:** Dominant criterion is **taste** — the jammy yolk turning into a warm dressing over cold, sharp salad is the whole hook, and it sits on a real nutrition base: eggs bring complete protein, choline and B12, spinach folate and iron, tomato vitamin C (which helps that iron absorb), all carried in olive oil (nutrition 🟢). One pot of boiling water and a knife make it effortless and all-supermarket (effort 🟢, availability 🟢).
