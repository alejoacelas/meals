## Tomato Egg-Drop Soup

Silky egg ribbons set into a tangy tomato broth in fifteen minutes — the fastest hot bowl in the kit, and the only soup that leans on eggs for its protein. It wins on effort: chop a little, simmer briefly, stream the egg in, done.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 2–3 eggs, beaten
- 2 ripe tomatoes, chopped (or ½ tin chopped tomatoes)
- ½ onion, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- a handful frozen peas
- ½ lemon
- a pinch of chilli flakes (optional)
- ~500ml water (boiling, to speed it up)
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Soften the onion in a glug of oil over medium heat, ~3 min; add the garlic and cook ~30s until fragrant.
2. Add the tomatoes and a pinch of salt; cook down 3–4 min to a loose, broken-down base.
3. Pour in the water and bring to a gentle simmer. Add the peas and simmer 2–3 min.
4. With the broth at a bare simmer, pour the beaten egg in a thin stream while stirring slowly in one direction — it sets into silky ribbons in seconds. Stop stirring and let it settle ~10s.
5. Off the heat, season with salt and plenty of black pepper, squeeze in lemon to taste, and add a pinch of chilli flakes if you want gentle heat.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Takeaway depth (+ soy sauce, + sesame oil, both not in kit):** a splash of soy off the heat and a few drops of sesame oil turn the plain broth into the savoury, nutty version everyone knows.
- **Fresh + warming finish (+ spring onion, + fresh ginger, both not in kit):** grate ginger in with the garlic for warmth; scatter sliced spring onion over the bowl for colour and a clean allium lift.
- **Silkier body (+ cornflour, not in kit):** stir a teaspoon of cornflour into a little cold water and whisk it into the simmering broth before the egg — it coats the spoon and holds the ribbons suspended.
- **More protein (+ silken or firm tofu, not in kit):** slide in cubes of tofu with the peas for extra protein and softness without muddying the broth.
