Firm tofu crumbled into golden, garlicky curds and loaded with mushroom, tomato and a fistful of wilted spinach, all piled onto seeded toast. The savoury, no-sugar, no-egg breakfast main — a hot protein-and-greens plate in ~20 minutes that pushes breakfast past the catalog's tomato-egg dishes.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- ~200g firm tofu (about ½ block), drained
- 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil (plus a splash more if needed)
- ½ tsp ground turmeric
- ½ tsp garlic granules
- 1 handful mushrooms (~100g), sliced
- 1 tomato, chopped
- 2 handfuls spinach (~50g)
- 2 slices wholemeal/seeded bread
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- A squeeze of fresh lemon
- Fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. Wrap the tofu in a clean cloth or paper towel and press out the water, then crumble it into rough, eggy curds.
2. Toast the bread — in a toaster, or dry-fry the slices in the pan 1–2 min a side, then set aside.
3. Heat the oil over medium-high. Add the tofu, turmeric, garlic granules and a good pinch of salt; fry, stirring now and then, 5–6 min until the curds are golden and a little drier at the edges.
4. Push the tofu aside, add the mushrooms (a splash more oil if dry) and cook 3–4 min until browned, then stir in the tomato and cook 2 min to soften.
5. Fold the spinach through in handfuls and let it wilt, ~1 min. Season well with salt and black pepper.
6. Pile onto the toast, scatter with chilli flakes, add a grind of pepper and a squeeze of lemon.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Truly eggy (+ kala namak / black salt, not in kit):** a pinch of sulphurous black salt at the end gives the real boiled-egg note tofu can't reach alone — the single biggest upgrade.
- **Cheesy depth + B12 (+ fortified nutritional yeast, not in kit):** a couple of spoonfuls stirred through for savoury, cheesy richness and the B12 plant proteins lack.
- **Richer plate (+ avocado, in kit; + soy sauce, not in kit):** fan sliced avocado over the toast and add a splash of soy to the pan for salty depth and good fat.
- **Fresh lift (+ spring onion or coriander, not in kit; + sriracha, not in kit):** scatter sliced spring onion or torn coriander and a drizzle of sriracha to wake the whole plate up.

**Why it scores well:** Nutrition is the win — tofu's complete protein, iron and calcium, spinach's folate and vitamin K, seeded bread's fibre and olive oil's good fat make a genuinely dense plate (black pepper helps the turmeric absorb). It's low effort too: one pan, one unbroken session — ~15 active minutes, ~20 to plate — all-supermarket (hence 🟢 availability).
