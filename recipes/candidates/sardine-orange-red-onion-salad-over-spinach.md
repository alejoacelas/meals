## Sardine, Orange & Red-Onion Salad over Spinach

The Sicilian move: sweet orange against oily fish. The citrus cuts the richness of the sardines and brightens the whole plate, and its vitamin C helps your body pull iron from the spinach and fish. An elegant no-cook dish — knife and bowl only.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 tin sardines (in oil or water), drained
- 1 orange
- ¼ red onion, very thinly sliced
- 2 big handfuls spinach (fresh; tear if leaves are large)
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- A pinch of chilli flakes
- Extra-virgin olive oil, fine sea salt, black pepper

**Method**
1. In your serving bowl, toss the sliced onion with the vinegar and a pinch of salt. Leave it to soften and lose its raw bite while you do the rest.
2. Segment the orange: slice off the top and bottom, stand it up and cut the peel and white pith away in strips, then cut between the membranes to free the segments (or just slice into thin rounds). Save any juice.
3. Pile the spinach onto a plate or wide bowl and dress with a good glug of olive oil, a little of the saved orange juice, salt and black pepper. Toss to coat and lightly wilt.
4. Lay the orange and the macerated onion over the spinach, then flake the sardines on top. Finish with another drizzle of oil, the chilli flakes and plenty of black pepper.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Sicilian classic (+ shaved fennel, not in kit):** shave a little raw fennel through for the traditional aniseed crunch that pairs with orange.
- **Brine & mint (+ olives and fresh mint, not in kit):** scatter black olives and torn mint for a salty, fragrant lift.
- **Crunch on top (+ toasted almonds or pumpkin seeds, in kit):** toast a handful in a dry pan and scatter over for texture and extra healthy fat.
- **Jewelled finish (+ pomegranate seeds, not in kit):** a handful adds pops of sweet-tart juice and colour.
