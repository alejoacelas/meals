Pressed tofu cubed and shallow-fried until every face genuinely crackles — no coating, just the tofu's own crust — then tossed off the heat in smoked paprika and garlic granules so the spice clings, all piled on garlicky chilli greens and showered with toasted pumpkin seeds. The flagship crispy pan-fried tofu: it proves the texture is achievable kit-only, smoky-crisp protein against bright greens with no starch in sight.

### Core recipe — proven ingredients only
**Ingredients**
- 1 block firm tofu (~280–400g)
- Rapeseed oil, for shallow-frying
- A small handful pumpkin seeds
- 1 tsp smoked (or sweet) paprika
- 1 tsp garlic granules
- 1 bunch kale, stems stripped, torn
- 2 big handfuls spinach
- 3 cloves garlic, sliced
- A good pinch chilli flakes
- Extra-virgin olive oil
- ½ lemon
- Fine sea salt and black pepper

**Method**
1. Drain the tofu, wrap it in a clean tea towel or kitchen paper, sit a plate with a weight (a tin or full pan) on top, and press 15–20 min while you prep the rest. Cut into 2cm cubes and pat dry.
2. Toast the pumpkin seeds in the dry pan over medium heat, tossing, until they pop and colour, ~3 min. Tip out, season with a pinch of salt.
3. Add a generous layer of rapeseed oil and set over medium-high. Lay the tofu in one layer and — this is the whole trick — leave each face undisturbed until deep golden and crisp before turning, ~10 min for all sides. Lift onto kitchen paper.
4. Off the heat, tip the hot tofu into a bowl and toss with the smoked paprika, garlic granules, salt and black pepper so the spice clings to the crackly surface.
5. Pour most of the oil from the pan, add a splash of olive oil, the garlic and chilli flakes, and sizzle 20 sec. Add the kale with a splash of water; wilt 2 min. Add the spinach and wilt ~1 min more. Squeeze in lemon, season with salt.
6. Pile the greens onto a plate, heap the tofu on top, shower with pumpkin seeds and a final squeeze of lemon.

### Level it up (optional — beyond the kit, widely available)
- **Sticky glaze (+ soy sauce + honey, not in kit):** off the heat, toss the crisp tofu in a splash of soy and a drizzle of honey instead of the dry spice for hot-honey, lacquered cubes.
- **Max crunch (+ cornflour, not in kit):** dust the pressed cubes in a thin film of cornflour before frying for a shatter-crisp shell that stays crunchy longer.
- **Nutty top (+ sesame seeds or tahini, not in kit):** scatter toasted sesame over the seeds, or loosen tahini with lemon and water into a drizzle over the greens.
- **Make it a plate (+ rice or warm flatbread, not in kit):** serve over rice or with flatbread to mop up the garlicky, paprika-stained oil.

**Why it scores well:** Dominant criterion is **nutrition** — two protein sources on one plate (firm tofu plus pumpkin seeds, which also bring magnesium, zinc and healthy fat), iron and calcium from the tofu, and a heavy load of vitamin K, C, folate and fibre from the kale and spinach, all carried in good oils with no starch padding it out (nutrition 🟢). Taste is strong too: crisp, smoky tofu against bright garlicky greens (taste 🟢). Every core item is an ordinary supermarket staple (availability 🟢). It's one pan and one session, but pressing the tofu and frying it patiently to a real crust make it the most hands-on of the one-pan dinners (effort 🟡).
